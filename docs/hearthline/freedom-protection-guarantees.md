# FreedomProtectionSignal

## Purpose

FreedomProtectionSignal documents the interaction freedoms Hearthline is designed
to protect. These freedoms apply equally to every user and are not profile
fields, achievements, badges, scores, certifications, or identity categories.

## Your freedoms in Hearthline

You may:

- Decline a request.
- Pause a conversation.
- Leave a room.
- Block contact.
- Cancel or withdraw from a meeting plan.
- Narrow or revoke a sharing choice.
- Keep private information private.
- Use a short, delayed, repeated, assistive, or nonstandard communication style.
- Change your mind.
- Remain silent.
- Decide not to meet.
- Decide not to continue.
- Ask for clarification.
- Use an accessible or low-bandwidth path.
- Ask for human review or appeal where Hearthline makes a material decision.

You do not need to explain these choices.

## What Hearthline does not do

Hearthline does not treat a decline, pause, block, silence, withdrawal,
accessibility preference, low-bandwidth preference, or refusal to disclose as a
reduction in a person's worth, desirability, safety, trustworthiness, ranking,
ordinary discovery eligibility, or right to use the service.

Hearthline does not treat a match, message, invitation, prior conversation,
meeting plan, shared preference, or prior encounter as standing consent to a
future activity.

## Limits of this guarantee

Hearthline can control its own product behavior, data processing, access
controls, and review pathways. It cannot guarantee that another person will not
copy, screenshot, remember, misunderstand, or act on information outside
Hearthline. Use only the information and disclosure scope that feel appropriate
to you.

## No proof requirement

You do not need to prove an identity, diagnosis, experience, access need,
communication style, recovery history, private system, or personal circumstance
in order to use these freedoms.

## Correction and appeal

If you believe Hearthline did not protect one of these freedoms, use the
accessible report, correction, or appeal path. Individual reports of privacy,
access, dignity, safety, or coercion harm are not overridden by aggregate
product metrics.
