# AcceptancePatternLibrary

## Purpose

AcceptancePatternLibrary is a static, plain-language collection of optional
social interaction patterns that support privacy, self-description, accessible
communication, boundaries, autonomy, non-escalation, non-interference, and
dignity in adult connection.

The Library is not a conduct score, safety score, identity test, legal guide,
medical guide, social-worth test, required curriculum, public badge system, or
eligibility condition.

## Pattern: Respect self-description

### Description

A person may describe themselves, their communication preferences, their
interests, and their boundaries in their own words. Respect does not require
proof, diagnosis, registration, technical inventory, institutional validation,
or public disclosure.

### Protects

- Autonomy.
- Privacy.
- Equal access.
- Freedom from identity inference.
- Freedom from disclosure pressure.

### Applies to

- Profiles.
- Story chapters.
- Direct conversations.
- Room participation.
- Accessibility preferences.
- Relationship and communication preferences.

### Examples

- “Thank you for telling me how you prefer to communicate.”
- “You can share only what you want to share.”
- “I understand that you prefer to keep that private.”
- “I will not infer more than you said.”

### Limits

This pattern does not require anyone to reveal identity, history, health,
recovery, private technology, relationship history, location, finances, or
intimate preferences. It does not make a personal statement a verification,
credential, legal status, diagnosis, or institutional determination.

## Pattern: Accept no without explanation

### Description

A person may decline, pause, block, leave, withdraw, or remain silent without
providing a reason. A boundary does not become less valid because it is brief,
late, repeated, nonstandard, or not explained.

### Protects

- Consent.
- Privacy.
- Non-interference.
- Freedom from retaliation.
- Safe exit.

### Applies to

- Direct messages.
- Invitations.
- Meeting plans.
- Room participation.
- Private discussion.
- Any request for more disclosure, contact, travel, intimacy, or care.

### Examples

- “No problem.”
- “Thank you for letting me know.”
- “You do not need to explain.”
- “I will not contact you again.”

### Limits

This pattern does not require continued friendship, forgiveness, repair,
agreement, emotional labor, or future access.

## Pattern: Separate interest from entitlement

### Description

Interest, attraction, attention, conversation, matching, prior contact, a
meeting plan, affection, or a previous encounter does not create entitlement
to a reply, more access, disclosure, travel, touch, intimacy, care, money,
gifts, housing, transport, forgiveness, or continued contact.

### Protects

- Consent.
- Bodily integrity.
- Privacy.
- Non-commercial integrity.
- Non-interference.

### Examples

- “I am interested, but I am not ready to plan anything.”
- “A match is not a promise.”
- “I can change my mind.”
- “I do not exchange resources for intimacy.”

### Limits

This pattern does not decide anyone’s legal status or create a legal contract.
