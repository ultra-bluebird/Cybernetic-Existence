# SexualHealthDialogueBridge and IntimacyEthicsCompass Research Contract

## Primary promises

SexualHealthDialogueBridge:
Enable two adults who both choose to open a private, temporary conversation to
use optional prompts about safer-sex communication, barriers, privacy, pace,
substance boundaries, and whether they want to discuss relevant information,
without requiring health disclosure, providing medical advice, creating a health
record, verifying testing, rating safety, creating consent, or authorizing a
real-world act.

IntimacyEthicsCompass:
Provide optional, static, plain-language reflection material about consent,
honesty, pace, privacy, boundaries, affection, aftercare, exit, and
non-commercial integrity without functioning as legal advice, medical advice,
a consent certificate, a moral score, a relationship rulebook, a user
classification, or an enforcement mechanism.

## SexualHealthDialogueBridge constraints

- The dialogue opens only after both adults separately choose to open it.
- Every prompt is optional, current, recipient-scoped, revocable, and may use
  `not_discussing_now` or `prefer_not_to_share`.
- The initial prompt set is limited to discussion readiness, barrier or
  protection preferences, health-information conversation preferences,
  comfort level, privacy preference, substance boundary, and pause/exit.
- The system does not collect or structure test results, test dates,
  diagnoses, symptoms, medications, treatment, providers, clinics, records,
  certificates, health histories, sexual history, partner history, pregnancy,
  fertility, disability, recovery, or substance-use history.
- The system does not provide medical advice, risk calculation, health
  verification, clinical triage, diagnosis, treatment guidance, or a safety
  rating.
- Shared prompt values never create consent, legal status, health status,
  meeting approval, intimacy approval, or permission for a real-world act.
- Values and outputs never enter discovery, ranking, matching, advertising,
  analytics, model training, moderation scoring, profile fields, or search.
- Either participant can pause, revoke, expire, or leave without explanation.
- The dialogue has no money, gift-conditioned, housing-conditioned,
  transport-conditioned, work-conditioned, debt-conditioned, drugs-for-
  intimacy, goods-for-intimacy, services-for-intimacy, or other
  value-for-intimacy path.
- Any later resource link is static and user-opened, not triggered by prompt
  selection or inferred need.

## IntimacyEthicsCompass constraints

- The Compass is static, read-only, versioned, non-personalized, and
  non-tracking.
- It is optional reflection material, not a required course, acknowledgement,
  quiz, certificate, badge, pledge, score, profile field, eligibility gate, or
  feature prerequisite.
- It states clearly that it is not legal advice, medical advice, diagnosis,
  consent certification, safety guarantee, or a rule that every adult must
  follow in the same way.
- It does not personalize guidance based on identity, sexuality, health,
  recovery, finances, location, body, relationship style, behavior, or
  inferred vulnerability.
- It does not route users into reporting, moderation, outside contact, health
  services, law enforcement, or emergency systems automatically.
- It has keyboard, screen-reader, text-resize, print, high-contrast,
  reduced-motion, plain-language, and low-bandwidth equivalents.

## Always-stop conditions

- A dialogue requires health disclosure, testing proof, a result, a diagnosis,
  a record, a provider, a medication, a treatment, or a health category.
- A dialogue output is presented as medical advice, medical verification, a
  safety rating, a consent record, legal compliance, or permission for a
  real-world act.
- Dialogue data becomes public, searchable, indexed, matched, ranked,
  advertised, analyzed, retained for model training, used for moderation
  scoring, or used to alter discovery, eligibility, or profile visibility.
- A participant cannot immediately pause, revoke, expire, or leave without
  explanation.
- The Compass becomes personalized, tracked, required, scored, certified,
  acknowledged, gated, or used for discovery, ranking, advertising,
  moderation, eligibility, or behavior classification.
- Either object creates pressure to disclose, meet, touch, become intimate,
  continue contact, provide aftercare, use a substance, or accept a
  value-for-intimacy arrangement.
- An accessibility-equivalent path is unavailable.
- Claims about privacy, encryption, deletion, health, consent, safety, or law
  exceed verified implementation or qualified-source evidence.

## Permitted measures

- Synthetic state-machine, revocation, expiry, and no-discovery-use tests.
- Accessibility task-completion tests using fictional accounts and prompts.
- Static Compass version and accessibility review outcomes.
- Voluntary feedback about clarity, pressure, accessibility, privacy, and
  non-moralizing language using fictional scenarios where preferred.
- Security review outcomes using synthetic dialogue data only.

## Prohibited measures

- Health inference.
- Testing verification.
- Medical record collection.
- Risk scoring.
- Consent prediction.
- Sexual activity prediction.
- Identity, sexuality, recovery, disability, financial, housing, capacity, or
  vulnerability inference.
- Discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation, eligibility, or behavioral
  profiling effects.
- Unconsented private-message, image, voice, or behavior analysis.
