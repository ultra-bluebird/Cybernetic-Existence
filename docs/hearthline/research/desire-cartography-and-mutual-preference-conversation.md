# DesireCartography and MutualPreferenceConversation Research Contract

## Primary promises

DesireCartography:
Give an adult a private, local-only, self-authored space to map changing
desires, boundaries, curiosities, pace, affection, privacy, rest, and
non-disclosure without creating a public inventory, partner record, match
input, score, diagnosis, prediction, consent record, or platform profile.

MutualPreferenceConversation:
Enable two adults who both choose to open a private conversation to share
selected current discussion preferences, receive non-scoring conversation
context, pause or revoke access, and keep the feature separate from discovery,
ranking, consent certification, meeting access, payment, and external action.

## DesireCartography constraints

- Entries are local-only, network-disabled, user-owned, and user-deletable.
- The default retention is disabled or session-only.
- Entries never contain counterpart, profile, room, corridor, meeting, message,
  location, address, workplace, identity, health, recovery, sexuality,
  financial, housing, employment, body, or private-intimacy references.
- The object does not infer or calculate orientation, gender, sexual role,
  desire level, relationship readiness, consent, health status, recovery,
  vulnerability, social confidence, desirability, wellness, or future behavior.
- Entries are never public, searchable, indexed, ranked, matched, advertised,
  used for moderation, used for analytics, used for model training, or used to
  change Hearthline behavior.
- A user may record uncertainty, no desire, no label, no change, rest, privacy,
  pause, or a changed mind.
- Export occurs only through an explicit user action and includes a local-file
  privacy warning.

## MutualPreferenceConversation constraints

- The object exists only in a current, mutual two-adult conversation context.
- Both participants separately choose to open the feature before any dimension
  can be shared.
- Every dimension is optional, recipient-scoped, current, revocable, and may
  use `not_discussing_yet`.
- The permitted initial dimensions are discussion readiness, pace, affection
  discussion, boundary style, safer-sex conversation, aftercare preference,
  privacy preference, and substance boundary.
- The object uses only `shared`, `different`, `not_shared`, and
  `not_discussing` relation labels.
- It produces conversation prompts, never percentages, scores, compatibility
  verdicts, recommendations, warnings, red/green flags, consent status, or
  readiness labels.
- Sharing a preference does not imply consent to touch, sexual activity,
  meeting, private venue, image exchange, travel, aftercare, future contact, or
  any real-world act.
- The object cannot unlock any platform capability.
- Values and outputs are excluded from discovery, ranking, advertising,
  analytics, model training, moderation scoring, profile fields, and search.
- Revocation and expiry remove ordinary Hearthline access but cannot guarantee
  that a recipient did not retain information outside the platform.
- The feature contains no money, rates, deposits, gifts conditioned on
  intimacy, housing, lodging, transport, work, debt relief, drugs, goods,
  services, or other value-for-intimacy path.

## Always-stop conditions

- Cartography content leaves the device, enters logs, synchronizes, is used for
  analytics, or changes platform behavior.
- Cartography is linked to another person, profile, message, room, meeting,
  location, or user account relationship.
- Cartography creates a sexual, identity, health, recovery, wellness,
  confidence, desirability, compatibility, consent, or relationship score.
- Mutual preference data becomes public, searchable, indexed, matched, ranked,
  advertised, analyzed, or used to change eligibility or discovery.
- A comparison output becomes a numeric score, compatibility percentage,
  recommendation, readiness label, consent record, legal statement, or
  real-world permission claim.
- A user cannot immediately pause, remove, revoke, expire, or leave the
  conversation without explanation.
- A user is pressured to share a dimension, provide a role, disclose a body
  detail, disclose a health detail, meet, travel, host, receive a ride, provide
  aftercare, become intimate, or continue contact.
- Any payment, gift, housing, lodging, transport, work, debt, drugs, goods,
  services, or valuable consideration becomes linked to intimacy.
- An accessibility-equivalent path is unavailable for open, share, edit, pause,
  revoke, delete, and expiry controls.
- Claims of encryption, deletion, ephemerality, confidentiality, or
  unrecoverability exceed verified implementation evidence.

## Permitted measures

- Synthetic local-only network-isolation tests for DesireCartography.
- Synthetic state-machine and revocation tests for MutualPreferenceConversation.
- Accessibility task-completion tests using fictional accounts and declarations.
- Voluntary feedback about clarity, privacy, pace, revocation, and pressure.
- Security review results using synthetic test content only.

## Prohibited measures

- Private desire-content analysis.
- Sexual preference inference.
- Compatibility scoring.
- User-level role, health, recovery, identity, body, intimacy, or behavior
  profiling.
- Discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation, or eligibility effects.
- Real-world encounter outcome prediction.
- Unconsented message or private-content scanning.
