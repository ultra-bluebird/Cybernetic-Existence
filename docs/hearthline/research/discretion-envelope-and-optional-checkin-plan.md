# DiscretionEnvelope and Optional Check-In Plan Research Contract

## Primary promises

DiscretionEnvelope:
Enable adults in a current mutual conversation to share selected, temporary,
revocable privacy preferences without creating a confidentiality contract,
silence requirement, consent record, legal agreement, reporting restriction,
trust score, meeting authorization, or claim that Hearthline can prevent
off-platform copying or disclosure.

Optional Check-In Plan:
Explore whether adults can configure a neutral, trusted-contact missed-check-in
reminder without creating platform surveillance, location tracking, emergency
monitoring, danger detection, safety certification, automatic reporting, or
automatic external escalation.

## DiscretionEnvelope constraints

- The user-facing object is private sharing preferences, not a confidentiality
  contract or secrecy agreement.
- Each participant independently shares their own current preference.
- A preference may be `okay_to_discuss_here`, `please_keep_private`,
  `temporary_context`, `not_discussing`, or `prefer_not_to_label`.
- Every preference is optional, recipient-scoped, revocable, and short-lived.
- A shared preference cannot restrict reporting, blocking, leaving, appeal,
  support seeking, privacy control, or communication with trusted people.
- The object does not require both people to agree before a meeting and does
  not authorize a meeting, touch, intimacy, privacy, or future contact.
- The object contains no location, address, workplace, route, health, recovery,
  identity, sexual activity, body, financial, housing, employment, criminal,
  emergency-contact, or third-party data.
- The object cannot unlock a capability or affect discovery, ranking,
  advertising, moderation, eligibility, or profiling.
- Hearthline states truthfully that it can remove ordinary in-platform access
  after revocation or expiry but cannot guarantee that someone did not copy,
  screenshot, remember, or disclose information outside the platform.

## Optional Check-In Plan constraints

- Research begins with synthetic contacts, synthetic events, and static
  notification previews only.
- No real contact information, location, meeting detail, counterpart data,
  identity, health, intimate context, route, transport, or emergency data is
  collected during initial research.
- Any future user-configured notification must use the minimum user-selected
  content and make no claim of danger, emergency, monitoring, tracking,
  response, delivery guarantee, or safety certification.
- No automatic contact occurs without prior explicit user configuration.
- No automatic emergency-service, police, employer, family, venue,
  counterpart, or platform-report contact is permitted.
- The plan cannot create a safety score, risk score, discovery effect,
  moderation effect, advertising signal, behavioral profile, or external
  action beyond the explicit, user-configured neutral notification.
- The plan must be cancellable, revocable, expiring, accessible, and
  understandable without requiring disclosure of why it is used.

## Always-stop conditions

- Discretion preferences become a confidentiality contract, non-reporting
  condition, consent record, legal statement, safety guarantee, or restriction
  on a participant's access to support.
- Discretion data becomes public, searchable, indexed, matched, ranked,
  advertised, analyzed, used for model training, used for moderation scoring,
  or linked to a profile/discovery/eligibility system.
- A participant cannot pause, revoke, expire, or leave without explanation.
- The platform claims or implies it can prevent copying, screenshots,
  off-platform disclosure, or memory.
- A check-in plan collects or reveals unnecessary contact, location, meeting,
  counterpart, intimate, health, identity, route, or emergency information.
- A notification claims danger, emergency, tracking, monitoring, or guaranteed
  help.
- A check-in plan automatically contacts emergency services, police, family,
  employer, venue, counterpart, authorities, or other third parties.
- A feature creates pressure to meet, disclose, travel, stay, accept a ride,
  host, become intimate, continue contact, or accept money or other value.
- An accessibility-equivalent control path is unavailable.
- Privacy, security, delivery, deletion, or notification claims exceed
  verified implementation evidence.

## Permitted measures

- Synthetic state-machine, expiry, revocation, and no-discovery-use tests.
- Accessibility tests using fictional privacy preferences and check-in plans.
- Static notification-preview comprehension testing.
- Voluntary feedback about pressure, privacy, cancellation, false-alarm
  interpretation, accessibility, and clarity.
- Security and privacy review outcomes using synthetic data only.

## Prohibited measures

- Real encounter monitoring.
- Location tracking.
- Contact behavior analytics.
- Safety or risk scoring.
- Identity, health, recovery, sexuality, financial, housing, capacity, or
  vulnerability inference.
- Discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation, eligibility, or behavioral
  profiling effects.
- Unconsented private-message, image, voice, device, or behavior analysis.
