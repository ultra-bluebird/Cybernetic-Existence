# Optional Mutual Encouragement Circle Research Protocol

## Purpose

This protocol evaluates whether a small, voluntarily formed group can use
optional encouragement, quiet witnessing, public activity context, and
non-clinical mutual presence without becoming therapy, crisis response,
monitoring, case management, a care-debt system, an informal reputation system,
or a mandatory support network.

## Research status

Research-design-only. No real group implementation, no real personal care
records, no emergency contact collection, and no clinical or crisis claims.

## Initial synthetic prototype

Use fictional members, fictional group charters, fictional encouragement notes,
fictional exits, and fictional public activity prompts.

Do not use:
- Real group memberships.
- Real health, recovery, identity, financial, housing, employment, legal, or
  crisis information.
- Real emergency contacts.
- Real member locations.
- Real check-in histories.
- Real messages.
- Real care obligations.
- Real assistance or resource exchanges.

## Allowed prototype activities

- Optional “thinking of you; no reply needed” note.
- Optional “quiet presence” room with no attendance record.
- Optional public activity suggestion with no transport, hosting, payment, or
  intimacy expectation.
- Optional member exit, pause, or charter revision.
- Static public resource link sharing.

## Prohibited behavior

- Therapy, diagnosis, treatment, recovery program, crisis intervention, or
  professional/peer-support certification.
- Mandatory attendance, response, check-in, care, disclosure, meeting, or
  emotional labor.
- Attendance scoring, reliability scoring, care debt, contribution score,
  influence score, or reputation.
- Emergency monitoring, automatic welfare checks, emergency contacts, location
  tracking, or automatic outside contact.
- Payment, gifts with conditions, housing, transport, work, debt relief, drugs,
  goods, services, or value-for-intimacy exchange.
- Discovery ranking, matching, advertising, moderation scoring, eligibility,
  or profile effects.
- Pressure to remain in the group or explain departure.

## Required user-facing statement

"This is an optional mutual encouragement space. It is not therapy, crisis
support, monitoring, emergency response, case management, or an obligation to
reply, disclose, care for someone, or remain in the group."

## Stop conditions

- Participants interpret the Circle as therapy, crisis support, emergency
  monitoring, mandatory support, or a substitute for professional care.
- A member is pressured to disclose, respond, attend, provide care, meet,
  travel, host, accept a ride, become intimate, or continue membership.
- Group membership creates a reputation, reliability, care, influence, or
  contribution score.
- The group creates a location, health, recovery, identity, financial,
  housing, employment, crisis, or emergency-contact record.
- A member cannot leave, pause, mute, or revise a group boundary without
  explanation.
- A group activity becomes tied to money, gifts, housing, transport, work,
  debt, drugs, goods, services, or value for intimacy.
- An accessibility-equivalent group, exit, mute, text-first, keyboard,
  screen-reader, low-bandwidth, and asynchronous path is unavailable.

## Advancement requirements

Advance only after:
- participant-protection review;
- privacy/security review;
- group coercion and power-asymmetry threat model;
- accessibility review;
- human-review and accessible grievance path;
- retention/deletion model;
- non-commercial-integrity review;
- synthetic evidence that exit, mute, pause, and no-reply paths carry no
  penalty;
- a clear decision that the platform can support the object without making
  clinical, emergency, or surveillance claims.
