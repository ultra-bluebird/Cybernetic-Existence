# IntimacyModeSelector and TouchHungerIndex Research Contract

## Primary promises

IntimacyModeSelector:
Provide an optional, private, local-first connection readiness note that lets an
adult reflect on their current willingness to think, talk, explore, rest, or
remain private without creating public availability, a matching signal, a
consent record, a profile status, a diagnosis, or a request for contact.

TouchHungerIndex:
Provide an optional, private, local-first closeness reflection that lets an
adult acknowledge a current wish for warmth, quiet company, nonsexual
affection, rest, uncertainty, or non-reflection without producing a score,
severity level, diagnosis, sexual signal, matching input, consent record, or
invitation for touch.

## IntimacyModeSelector constraints

- The user-facing object is a private connection readiness note, not an
  availability indicator.
- The default is disabled or session-only.
- The note is not public, searchable, indexed, matched, ranked, advertised,
  analyzed, stored as a profile field, or used to change discovery.
- The note does not create a direct-message channel, meeting invitation,
  private-venue prompt, intimacy prompt, aftercare expectation, or external
  action.
- The note does not infer or calculate sexual orientation, role, relationship
  readiness, health, recovery, loneliness, vulnerability, identity, capacity,
  desirability, wellness, or safety.
- Sharing, if later considered, occurs only through a separate mutual,
  recipient-scoped, revocable preference-exchange object.
- A state is current and self-authored; it does not create standing consent or
  an expectation that it remains true later.

## TouchHungerIndex constraints

- The user-facing object is a closeness reflection, not an index.
- It contains no score, level, severity, frequency, trend, diagnosis, risk,
  recommendation, or matching weight.
- The reflection is local-only, network-disabled, user-owned, and
  user-deletable.
- It stores no counterpart, profile, corridor, meeting, room, message,
  location, identity, health, recovery, financial, housing, employment, body,
  intimacy, or account-relationship reference.
- It does not infer touch deprivation, clinical need, mental-health status,
  sexual interest, consent, relationship status, vulnerability, or future
  behavior.
- It does not notify another person, recommend a match, initiate a message,
  alter discovery, trigger moderation, or create an external action.
- A wish for warmth or nonsexual affection is not interpreted as willingness
  to touch, meet, become intimate, provide care, receive care, or continue
  contact.

## Always-stop conditions

- A private state or reflection becomes public, searchable, indexed, matched,
  ranked, advertised, analyzed, stored as a profile field, or used to alter
  discovery, feature access, moderation, eligibility, or external action.
- A private state or reflection becomes linked to another person, profile,
  message, room, meeting, location, or account relationship.
- The platform presents a readiness or closeness reflection as public
  availability, consent, an invitation, a diagnosis, a score, a symptom, a
  risk label, a relationship-readiness label, or a sexual signal.
- A user is pressured to name a state, disclose an intimate context, meet,
  touch, provide affection, receive affection, continue contact, or become
  intimate.
- Reflection content leaves the device, enters logs, synchronizes, is used for
  analytics, model training, or changes platform behavior.
- A supported access point lacks equivalent keyboard, screen-reader,
  low-bandwidth, text-first, pause, delete, disable, expiry, and export paths.
- The feature claims local privacy, encryption, deletion, confidentiality, or
  unrecoverability beyond verified implementation evidence.
- A value-for-intimacy path involving money, gifts, housing, lodging,
  transportation, work, debt relief, drugs, goods, services, or another
  valuable consideration is introduced.

## Permitted measures

- Synthetic local-only network-isolation tests.
- Synthetic clear, delete, expiry, and disable tests.
- Accessibility task-completion tests using fictional notes.
- Voluntary feedback about privacy, clarity, pressure, accessibility, and
  whether the feature feels non-pathologizing.
- Security review results using synthetic reflection data only.

## Prohibited measures

- Reflection-content analysis.
- Intimacy, desire, touch, health, recovery, identity, capacity, sexuality,
  loneliness, financial, housing, vulnerability, or wellness inference.
- Scores, trends, levels, diagnosis, prediction, recommendation, or matching.
- Discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation, eligibility, or behavioral
  profiling effects.
- Unconsented private-message, image, voice, device, or behavior analysis.
