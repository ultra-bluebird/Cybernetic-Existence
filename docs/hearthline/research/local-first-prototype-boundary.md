# Hearthline Local-First Prototype Boundary

## Objective

Prototype social and relationship objects without collecting more data than the
prototype needs, without creating a participant ranking system, and without
turning reflective tools into platform surveillance.

## Local-first requirements

- Draft messages remain in the browser unless the user explicitly sends them.
- ReaderPause, PressureBackstop, PatternMirror, CuriosityBudget, ReaderWeather,
  and private reflection tools execute locally.
- Discarded text, self-check responses, and pause durations are not transmitted.
- Private intimacy objects are excluded from discovery, analytics, advertising,
  recommendations, and fairness audits.
- Exact locations, work addresses, home addresses, payment handles, and external
  payment links are not accepted in connection-planning objects.
- No prototype sends messages, books venues, contacts third parties, moves money,
  or initiates off-platform actions.

## Research records

Only the following may enter aggregate research review:

- explicit participant feedback;
- user-visible feature completion events;
- aggregate accessibility test outcomes;
- aggregate stop-condition counts;
- documented research-lane closures.

No raw drafts, private stories, private sexual content, inferred identity,
precise location, private health information, recovery history, financial
information, or device fingerprints may enter the research record.

## Mandatory closure rule

If a stop condition or promise contradiction is observed, the relevant object
version closes. It cannot be reactivated. Any later work requires a new object
version, a revised protocol, renewed participant consent, and a new review.
