# EncounterComplianceLedger Research Contract

## Primary promise

Maintain a minimal, auditable record of Hearthline governance processes without
retaining intimate content, creating a person-level profile, assigning a score,
or allowing ledger data to affect discovery, dating, privacy, access, or
eligibility.

## Object constraints

- Ledger events contain only opaque case/event identifiers, event category,
  review status, outcome category, minimal timing, policy version, retention
  expiry, correction reference, access-purpose category, and integrity hash.
- The ledger stores no account, profile, identity, device, address, location,
  message, media, report text, private compatibility, afterglow, payment,
  health, recovery, financial, sexuality, or vulnerability data.
- Case-to-person or case-to-content mappings are held only in a separate,
  protected, purpose-limited review system and expire earlier than the ledger.
- The ledger is tamper-evident and correction-capable, not an immutable
  personal archive.
- Every ledger access is itself recorded with a closed access-purpose category.
- No product, matching, discovery, recommendation, advertising, analytics,
  payment, or model-training system may access the ledger.
- Ledger events never create a risk score, trust score, legal score, consent
  score, morality score, commercial-activity score, or reputation score.
- Retention closure destroys mappings and keys before any remaining aggregate
  marker is retained.
- The ledger cannot automatically contact law enforcement, employers, family,
  venues, emergency contacts, or other external parties.

## Always-stop conditions

- Sensitive content, user identity, account identifier, location, profile,
  message, health, recovery, finance, sexuality, vulnerability, or device
  information enters the ledger.
- Ledger data is joined with discovery, ranking, recommendation, advertising,
  analytics, model training, payments, or person-level moderation scoring.
- A ledger event is represented as proof of a person's intent, consent,
  commercial conduct, legal status, character, or future eligibility.
- The ledger becomes a permanent identifiable archive without an approved,
  purpose-limited retention and deletion path.
- A correction cannot be recorded without rewriting history, or a historic
  record is silently modified.
- Access occurs without a recorded approved purpose.
- A participant cannot access an accessible correction or appeal pathway where
  a material platform decision has been made.
- An external action is initiated from ledger data without separate, explicit
  authority and review.

## Permitted measures

- Aggregate governance-process counts.
- Aggregate appeal, correction, and reversal rates.
- Retention-closure completion results.
- Integrity-verification outcomes.
- Authorized-access audit outcomes.
- Accessibility test outcomes using synthetic cases.

## Prohibited measures

- User-level compliance profiles.
- Risk, trust, legality, consent, desirability, or morality scores.
- Discovery or matching effects.
- Advertiser audiences.
- Content or identity analysis.
- Private-message surveillance.
- Location, health, recovery, financial, identity, sexuality, capacity, or
  vulnerability inference.
