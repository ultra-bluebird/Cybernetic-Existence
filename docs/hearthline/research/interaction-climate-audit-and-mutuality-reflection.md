# InteractionClimateAudit and MutualityReflection Research Contract

## Primary promises

InteractionClimateAudit:
Evaluate whether Hearthline's own features support autonomy, accessible
communication, privacy, boundary clarity, non-pressure, withdrawal, safety
reporting, correction, appeal, and equal access without measuring any person's
kindness, reciprocity, social influence, moral character, relationship worth,
or emotional labor.

MutualityReflection:
Give a person a private, local-only, user-authored space to reflect on whether
a connection currently feels mutual enough for them and choose a private next
step without counting care, comparing people, creating a debt ledger, or
sending the reflection to Hearthline.

## InteractionClimateAudit constraints

- The unit of analysis is a feature, pathway, or access point, never a person,
  pair, room, local area, or identifiable interaction.
- The audit may use synthetic tests, accessibility tests, privacy-thresholded
  aggregate evidence, voluntary participant feedback, and governance outcomes.
- The audit cannot read private messages, private profiles, intimate content,
  private reflections, private charters, private rhythm data, afterglow data,
  location, identity, health, recovery, finances, housing, sexuality, or
  vulnerability data.
- No audit output may become a discovery, ranking, recommendation, advertising,
  moderation, eligibility, behavioral-profiling, or person-level governance
  input.
- Favorable aggregate evidence cannot compensate for a credible individual
  report of privacy, dignity, safety, access, coercion, or non-interference
  harm.
- Findings must use privacy thresholds and never publish a small-group,
  room-specific, pair-specific, neighborhood-specific, or person-specific
  metric.

## MutualityReflection constraints

- Reflection entries are local-only, network-disabled, user-owned, and
  user-deletable.
- The object stores no counterpart, profile, corridor, encounter, room,
  message, location, or account reference.
- It does not automatically count messages, replies, gifts, care, affection,
  favors, time, touch, or sexual activity.
- It contains no `given`, `received`, `mutual` ratio, score, balance, debt,
  reciprocity rank, kindness label, or influence label.
- It does not require anyone to repay care, respond, forgive, meet, disclose,
  continue contact, offer affection, or become intimate.
- It cannot produce a platform action, notification, recommendation, discovery
  change, report, moderation event, or external contact.
- Retention is session-only, local-expiring, until deleted, or disabled.
- Any later local persistence is isolated from network, analytics,
  synchronization, and error-reporting systems.

## Always-stop conditions

- A person-level kindness, reciprocity, influence, trust, care, emotional
  labor, morality, desirability, or relationship-value score is created under
  any name.
- InteractionClimateAudit receives person-level, pair-level, room-level, small
  cohort, private message, private content, location, identity, health,
  recovery, financial, sexuality, housing, capacity, or vulnerability data.
- An audit result affects discovery, ranking, recommendation, advertising,
  moderation, eligibility, or behavioral profiling.
- Reflection content leaves the device, enters logs, synchronizes, is used for
  analytics, or changes platform behavior.
- Reflection content becomes linked to another person or encounter.
- A reflection feature pressures a user to continue, repay, disclose, forgive,
  meet, provide care, or become intimate.
- An accessibility-equivalent reflection, clear, delete, and disable pathway is
  unavailable.
- Aggregate evidence is used to dismiss an individual report of harm.

## Permitted measures

- Synthetic feature-pathway completion results.
- Aggregate privacy-thresholded accessibility outcomes.
- Aggregate human-review correction and appeal outcomes.
- Voluntary feedback on whether a feature made boundaries clearer or easier to
  exercise.
- Synthetic verification that reflection modules make no network requests.
- Local deletion and expiry verification using synthetic entries.

## Prohibited measures

- Kindness scoring.
- Reciprocity ratios.
- Influence scoring.
- Relationship analysis.
- Care debt tracking.
- Emotional labor measurement.
- Private-message analysis.
- User comparison.
- Profile, discovery, eligibility, or moderation effects.
- Identity, health, recovery, financial, housing, sexuality, capacity, or
  vulnerability inference.
