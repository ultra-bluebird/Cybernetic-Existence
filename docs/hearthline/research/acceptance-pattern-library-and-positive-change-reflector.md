# AcceptancePatternLibrary and PositiveChangeReflector Research Contract

## Primary promises

AcceptancePatternLibrary:
Provide optional, plain-language, versioned examples of rights-respecting
social interaction without creating identity standards, behavioral compliance
requirements, public badges, moral scores, legal advice, medical advice, or
personalized judgments.

PositiveChangeReflector:
Provide a private, local-only, user-authored space for a person to notice,
describe, revise, or discard their own changing preferences and experiences
without producing a confidence score, growth score, relationship score,
wellness score, recovery score, comparison, inference, or platform action.

## AcceptancePatternLibrary constraints

- The Library is read-only, static, non-personalized, and non-tracking.
- Patterns are descriptive and optional, not mandatory rules or user tests.
- Patterns protect autonomy, privacy, accessible communication, self-description,
  consent, non-escalation, non-interference, safe exit, and non-commercial
  integrity.
- The Library does not assign a preferred identity, relationship style,
  communication style, recovery path, social trajectory, belief, or moral
  status.
- The Library does not require reading acknowledgement, quiz completion,
  certificate, badge, pledge, account verification, or profile disclosure.
- The Library cannot be used for discovery, ranking, recommendation,
  advertising, moderation scoring, eligibility, user classification, or
  behavioral profiling.
- Every pattern has a version, source note, review state, limits, and scheduled
  review date.
- The Library has keyboard, screen-reader, text-resize, print, plain-language,
  high-contrast, and reduced-motion equivalents.

## PositiveChangeReflector constraints

- All reflector entries are local-only, network-disabled, user-owned, and
  user-deletable.
- The default is disabled or session-only retention.
- The user may record no change, uncertainty, rest, pause, changed mind,
  preference not to answer, or any self-authored statement.
- The Reflector cannot calculate confidence, readiness, wellness, recovery,
  dating success, social skill, boundary quality, relationship quality,
  desirability, communication competence, attachment style, or future risk.
- Entries contain no counterpart, profile, corridor, meeting, room, message,
  location, identity, health, recovery, sexuality, financial, housing, or
  employment reference.
- Entries cannot trigger platform notifications, recommendations, discovery
  changes, moderation, reports, outside contact, or external action.
- Export happens only by explicit user action and contains a clear local-file
  privacy warning.
- Any later local persistence uses a storage adapter that is isolated from
  network, analytics, synchronization, session replay, and error reporting.

## Always-stop conditions

- Acceptance patterns become a required course, moral score, public badge,
  account gate, reading acknowledgement, personalized recommendation, or
  behavioral classification.
- A pattern is used to infer identity, health, recovery, capacity, sexuality,
  finances, vulnerability, belief, or relationship eligibility.
- Reflector content leaves the device, enters logs, is synchronized, is used
  for analytics, or changes platform behavior.
- Reflector entries become linked to another person, profile, room, meeting,
  message, location, or account relationship.
- The Reflector presents a score, trend, rank, diagnosis, prediction, or
  improvement judgment.
- A user feels pressured to record positive change, disclose a private history,
  date more, communicate more, meet more, forgive, continue contact, or become
  intimate.
- A supported access point lacks equivalent accessible paths to browse Library
  content or create, read, clear, disable, delete, and export reflector notes.
- Aggregate Library engagement or reflection usage is used to dismiss an
  individual privacy, dignity, access, safety, or coercion concern.

## Permitted measures

- Static content version publication and withdrawal.
- Accessibility evaluations using synthetic scenarios.
- Local-only network-isolation tests using synthetic reflection entries.
- Voluntary feedback about whether Library language is understandable and
  nonjudgmental.
- Voluntary feedback about whether the Reflector feels private, optional, and
  non-comparative.

## Prohibited measures

- Pattern-reading history.
- Knowledge scores.
- Values alignment scores.
- Growth metrics.
- Reflection-content analysis.
- User confidence, wellness, recovery, relationship, intimacy, or desirability
  inference.
- Discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation, eligibility, or behavioral
  profiling effects.
