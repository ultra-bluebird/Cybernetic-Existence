# SocialWellnessIndex and AssistanceNetworkIndex Research Contract

## Primary promises

SocialWellnessIndex:
Study Hearthline feature conditions in aggregate, with voluntary and
privacy-protected feedback, to understand whether autonomy, privacy control,
access equivalence, boundary clarity, noninterference, public-place access, and
appeal/correction pathways are functioning without creating individual or
community wellness scores, identity inference, user profiling, or a public
health claim.

AssistanceNetworkIndex:
Provide a static, source-labeled, user-opened public resources library for
adults who may independently browse transit, food, housing information, health
information, recovery information, legal information, employment information,
accessibility information, community information, and emergency information
without referral, tracking, profiling, need inference, eligibility
determination, appointment booking, or automatic outreach.

## SocialWellnessIndex constraints

- The user-facing object is a community experience study, not a wellness index.
- No composite score, ranking, trend score, public-health score, or individual
  score is calculated.
- Study dimensions remain separate and non-compensating.
- Participation and feedback are optional, purpose-limited, and not triggered
  by inferred need, identity, health, recovery, loneliness, encounter outcome,
  or private behavior.
- Research cohorts are access-path-based, synthetic, or voluntary; they are not
  inferred protected, identity, health, recovery, financial, housing,
  sexuality, capacity, or vulnerability cohorts.
- Results are aggregate-only, privacy-thresholded, delayed, and suppressed for
  small groups.
- Raw free-text feedback is not published.
- Study data cannot be joined to profiles, discovery, matching, ranking,
  advertising, moderation, eligibility, payments, resource browsing, or
  behavioral profiling.
- Favorable aggregate findings cannot override an individual report of privacy,
  access, dignity, coercion, safety, or non-interference harm.

## AssistanceNetworkIndex constraints

- The user-facing object is a public resources library, not a referral system,
  case-management system, eligibility tool, provider endorsement, or need
  assessment.
- Entries are static, versioned, source-labeled, jurisdiction-labeled,
  plain-language, reviewed, and removable.
- The library is user-opened, non-personalized, non-tracking, and available
  without profile completion or sensitive disclosure.
- Hearthline does not infer a need, push a resource, send user data to a
  provider, book an appointment, contact a provider, determine eligibility,
  determine availability, determine affordability, or guarantee suitability.
- Broad jurisdiction selection, if offered, is manual and not retained as
  account-linked location history.
- Resource categories may include transit, food, housing information, health
  information, recovery information, legal information, employment information,
  accessibility information, community information, and emergency information.
- The library clearly distinguishes official/public sources from third-party
  directories and includes source retrieval and review dates.
- Resource browsing cannot affect discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation,
  eligibility, payment, safety score, profile, or behavioral profile.

## Always-stop conditions

- A community experience score, individual score, wellness label, connection
  score, belonging score, safety score, public-health claim, or user profile
  is created.
- Survey feedback is required, inferred, linked to a profile, joined to
  discovery or behavior data, or used for ranking, advertising, moderation,
  eligibility, resource targeting, or profiling.
- Small-group or cohort outputs allow plausible re-identification.
- Aggregate positive results are used to dismiss an individual harm report.
- A resource library infers need, pushes a resource, requires sensitive
  disclosure, tracks browsing, shares user data, makes a referral, books a
  service, determines eligibility, claims provider availability, or claims
  that a resource is appropriate for a specific person.
- Resource browsing changes discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation,
  eligibility, payment, profile, or behavioral data.
- Entries are stale, unsourced, inaccessible, misleadingly current, or
  represented as legal, medical, emergency, or case-management advice.
- A supported access point lacks equivalent keyboard, screen-reader,
  low-bandwidth, text-first, browse, search, source-link, correction, and
  removal paths.
- A feature creates pressure to disclose, meet, travel, accept assistance,
  accept a ride, host, become intimate, continue contact, or accept money or
  other value.

## Permitted measures

- Synthetic aggregate-study configuration and suppression tests.
- Accessibility task-completion tests using fictional surveys and resources.
- Source freshness and review-date checks.
- Aggregate non-account-linked static-delivery health.
- Voluntary feedback about clarity, privacy, accessibility, and non-pressure.
- Aggregate correction, removal, and source-update outcomes.

## Prohibited measures

- Individual wellness scoring.
- Community ranking.
- Need inference.
- Resource recommendation based on profile or behavior.
- Resource click tracking.
- User location tracking.
- Identity, health, recovery, financial, housing, sexuality, capacity, or
  vulnerability inference.
- Discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation, eligibility, payment, or
  behavioral profiling effects.
- Unconsented message, image, voice, device, behavior, or location analysis.
