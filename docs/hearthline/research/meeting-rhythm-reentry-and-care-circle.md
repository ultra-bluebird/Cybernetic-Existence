# MeetingThresholdObject, SocialFrequencyTuner, EncounterReentryProtocol,
# and CommunityCareCircle Research Contract

## Primary promises

MeetingThresholdObject:
Provide a private, local-first meeting conditions note that helps an adult
remember their own current public-place, transit, privacy, substance, pace, and
exit preferences without creating a public demand, profile filter, counterparty
requirement, meeting invitation, or discovery signal.

SocialFrequencyTuner:
Provide a private, local-first social rhythm note that helps an adult reflect
on preferred communication frequency without creating a response quota,
availability status, social-capacity label, profile field, matching filter, or
deficiency judgment.

EncounterReentryProtocol:
Allow an adult to resume selected Hearthline features at their own pace after a
pause without shame, reactivation campaign, profile announcement, old-contact
revival, streak, abandonment label, discovery boost, discovery penalty, or
inference about why they paused.

CommunityCareCircle:
Research whether an optional, small, mutually formed encouragement space can
support non-clinical voluntary presence without creating therapy claims, crisis
response, monitoring, care debt, disclosure pressure, reputation, or group
obligation.

## MeetingThresholdObject constraints

- Meeting conditions are local-only, network-disabled, user-owned, and
  user-deletable.
- Conditions may cover public-first venue, broad time preference, transit-aware
  context, substance boundary, setting preference, privacy preference, exit
  preference, and meeting state.
- Conditions cannot include home, workplace, GPS, route, transit stop, calendar,
  routine, counterpart requirements, host status, ride provision, hotel,
  lodging, payment, gifts, financial status, health, identity, sexual
  condition, body information, safety score, or eligibility.
- The object cannot publish, search, match, rank, advertise, analyze, filter,
  profile, or impose conditions on another person.
- Sharing, if later considered, requires a separate mutual, recipient-scoped,
  revocable conversation object.

## SocialFrequencyTuner constraints

- Social rhythm is local-only, network-disabled, user-owned, and
  user-deletable.
- A rhythm does not create a reply expectation, response timer, recurring
  schedule, availability signal, inactive status, abandonment label, discovery
  effect, match filter, profile field, or health/capacity inference.
- A user may choose variable rhythm, rare contact, no label, or no reflection
  without penalty.
- The platform does not infer loneliness, isolation, recovery, disability,
  health, social skill, reliability, trustworthiness, or relationship quality
  from rhythm preferences or response timing.

## EncounterReentryProtocol constraints

- Reentry is user-initiated, private, and scope-limited.
- The platform does not send a welcome-back campaign, revive old contacts,
  announce reactivation, infer pause reason, record abandonment, create a
  streak, or create a discovery boost or penalty.
- Resuming a feature does not imply resuming meetings, intimacy, disclosure,
  private conversations, old plans, old grants, or old relationships.
- A user may resume only selected ordinary features or remain in quiet presence.
- Reentry does not create a public status, profile change, ranking change,
  advertising signal, analytics profile, or external action.

## CommunityCareCircle constraints

- CommunityCareCircle remains research-design-only until participant-protection,
  privacy/security, accessibility, non-commercial-integrity, group-power,
  retention, grievance, and exit-rights reviews are complete.
- Any future Circle is voluntarily formed, small, non-clinical, non-monitoring,
  non-emergency, non-commercial, and revocable.
- Membership is not searchable, rankable, scored, public by default, or used
  for discovery, matching, advertising, moderation, eligibility, or profile
  effects.
- No member owes a reply, attendance, disclosure, care, meeting, transport,
  housing, money, work, goods, services, intimacy, or continued membership.
- The Circle does not collect health, recovery, crisis, identity, financial,
  housing, employment, emergency contact, location, or real care-obligation
  records.
- A member can leave, pause, mute, or set a boundary without explanation or
  penalty.

## Always-stop conditions

- A local note leaves the device, enters logs, synchronizes, is used for
  analytics, model training, discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation,
  eligibility, or platform behavior.
- A private preference becomes public availability, a profile field, a filter,
  a requirement, a score, a status, or a counterpart obligation.
- A pause or reentry produces a campaign, old-contact revival, announcement,
  abandonment label, streak, boost, penalty, or inferred private circumstance.
- A user is pressured to meet, travel, host, accept a ride, disclose, respond,
  attend, care, become intimate, continue contact, or accept money or other
  value.
- A Circle becomes therapy, crisis response, monitoring, emergency support,
  case management, a reputation system, a care-debt system, or a mandatory
  support network.
- A group, preference, reentry, or rhythm state becomes linked to health,
  recovery, identity, finances, housing, employment, capacity, vulnerability,
  location, counterpart, or profile data.
- A supported access point lacks equivalent keyboard, screen-reader,
  low-bandwidth, text-first, asynchronous, pause, delete, clear, disable,
  expiry, reentry, exit, mute, and appeal paths.
- Any claim about privacy, encryption, deletion, safety, care, support,
  monitoring, or emergency response exceeds verified implementation evidence.
- A value-for-intimacy path involving money, gifts, housing, lodging,
  transportation, work, debt, drugs, goods, services, or other valuable
  consideration is introduced.

## Permitted measures

- Synthetic local-only network-isolation tests.
- Synthetic pause, reentry, no-campaign, no-contact-revival, and no-ranking
  tests.
- Accessibility task-completion tests using fictional notes and group fixtures.
- Synthetic group exit, mute, boundary, and no-reply tests.
- Voluntary feedback about clarity, pressure, privacy, accessibility, and
  non-clinical understanding.
- Security and participant-protection review outcomes using synthetic data only.

## Prohibited measures

- Frequency scoring.
- Social-capacity inference.
- Abandonment tracking.
- Reentry conversion optimization.
- Group attendance tracking.
- Care debt, influence, contribution, reliability, safety, or reputation
  scoring.
- Identity, health, recovery, financial, housing, employment, capacity,
  sexuality, or vulnerability inference.
- Discovery, ranking, advertising, moderation, eligibility, payment, or
  behavioral profiling effects.
- Unconsented message, image, voice, device, behavior, or location analysis.
