# IntimacyEthicsCompass

## Purpose

IntimacyEthicsCompass is an optional, plain-language reflection guide for
adults thinking about communication, privacy, consent, affection, pace,
aftercare, boundaries, non-commercial integrity, and exit.

It is not legal advice, medical advice, a diagnosis, a consent certificate, a
relationship rulebook, a moral score, a safety guarantee, or a requirement to
disclose anything personal.

## Consent remains current

A conversation, profile, message, match, shared preference, invitation,
meeting plan, prior encounter, or previous “yes” does not create standing
consent for a later activity.

People can pause, change their mind, say no, leave, block contact, or remain
silent. A person does not need to provide a reason.

## Honesty without disclosure pressure

You may say what you want, what you do not want, what you are unsure about, or
what you prefer to keep private. You do not have to disclose identity, health,
recovery, finances, living situation, private history, body details, location,
or any other personal information to be treated with respect.

## Pace and difference

People can want different timing, communication styles, amounts of discussion,
levels of privacy, forms of affection, or types of connection. Difference is
information, not a defect that must be negotiated away.

A respectful outcome may be continuing a conversation, slowing down, pausing,
choosing friendship, choosing a public activity, choosing no further contact,
or deciding not to meet.

## Affection is not escalation

Friendship, care, conversation, affection, a hug, cuddling, romance, sex, and
private time are distinct. Interest in one does not imply agreement to another.

## Privacy and sharing

Share only what you choose. Private information, screenshots, copied text, and
off-platform conversation may be retained by another person even when a
platform removes ordinary access. Consider what feels appropriate to share.

## Aftercare and exit

If an encounter or conversation occurs, people may have different preferences
about follow-up. A person may welcome a check-in, prefer quiet, prefer no
follow-up, or prefer to decide later.

No one is required to provide emotional labor, forgiveness, explanation,
continued contact, affection, or intimacy.

## Non-commercial integrity

Hearthline does not support money, rates, deposits, payment links, tips, gifts
with conditions, housing, lodging, transport, work, debt relief, goods,
services, drugs, or other valuable consideration linked to intimacy.

Ordinary kindness, friendship, a shared activity, and independent decisions
about personal expenses are not made into platform transactions.

## When medical information matters

Hearthline can support respectful conversation prompts, but it does not provide
medical advice, testing advice, diagnosis, health verification, or treatment
guidance. For medical questions, symptoms, testing, prevention, treatment, or
urgent health concerns, consider a qualified healthcare professional or an
appropriate local health service.
