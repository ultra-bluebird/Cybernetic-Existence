# Phoenix Civic Access Legal Research Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix distinguishes verified public legal and administrative sources from Augmented-Hub project governance requirements. It supports accessible, privacy-preserving civic technology design and does not establish legal advice, municipal authority, clinical status, or a new protected legal classification.

## Source Categories

| Category | Definition | Minimum Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed law or regulation | Enacted statute, regulation, binding code, or official rule | Official publisher, citation, current text, retrieval date |
| Confirmed municipal policy or program | Published Phoenix policy, program, procedure, budget action, or administrative material | Official City publication and scope statement |
| Project governance requirement | Augmented-Hub technical or ethical requirement | Approved project artifact and version |
| Legal compatibility analysis | Source-based assessment of a project requirement against law or policy | Linked primary sources and stated limitation |
| Open research question | Matter requiring further authoritative verification | Research plan and source gap |

## Non-Negotiable Invariants

- Do not describe “cybernetic host” or “augmented citizen” as existing Phoenix, Arizona, or federal legal classifications without authoritative text.
- Do not collect neural data, biometric data, health data, device telemetry, or a private narrative unless strictly necessary, explicitly consented, purpose-limited, and protected by retention and deletion controls.
- Do not make dignity, essential-service access, accommodation review, or assistive access dependent on external identity certification.
- Do not use community recognition, trust signals, or governance participation as a social score or eligibility mechanism.
- Do not permit a material access, augmentation, or continuity-related change without accessible explanation, explicit revocable consent, human review, and appeal.
- Do not write private claimant information to a public ledger, source manifest, or public audit log.
- Do not activate any public-benefit ledger until verified authority, appropriation, program administration, audit oversight, and procurement/payment controls are documented.
- Do not treat this matrix as legal advice; verify applicability, currentness, and implementation obligations with qualified jurisdiction-specific review.

## Evidence Record Template

| Field | Required Value |
|---|---|
| Public source reference | Stable internal source reference |
| Authority | City of Phoenix, Arizona Legislature, U.S. Department of Justice, or another authoritative issuer |
| Official title | Exact title from official publication |
| Official URL | HTTPS publication link |
| Source category | Confirmed law or regulation, municipal policy, project policy, compatibility analysis, or open question |
| Published, adopted, or effective date | Date from authoritative source where available |
| Retrieval date | Date the source was retrieved |
| Applicability scope | Program, service, activity, building, employment, procurement, or digital-service scope |
| Content commitment | SHA-256 of the retrieved authoritative document |
| Implementation implication | Specific system, policy, interface, workflow, or test requirement |
| Limitation | Jurisdiction, factual, technical, or legal uncertainty that remains |
