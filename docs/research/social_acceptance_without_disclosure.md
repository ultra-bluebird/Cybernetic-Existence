# Measuring Institutional Inclusion Without Identity Disclosure

## Research Question

How can public institutions measure changes in inclusion toward cybernetic hosts and augmented citizens while requiring zero disclosure of augmentation status from individuals?

## Scope

This research measures institutional and public conditions only.

It does not measure identity legitimacy, individual acceptance, personal worth, disability, health status, augmentation status, political belief, or service eligibility.

## Inputs

- Public policy documents
- Public accessibility and privacy notices
- Public consultation records
- Aggregate complaint and appeal process metrics
- Aggregate accessibility-service availability audits
- Publicly documented correction and grievance pathways

## Prohibited Inputs

- Individual identity disclosures
- Cybernetic or assistive-device status
- Medical records
- Employment, education, housing, or transit histories
- Biometric data
- Face, voice, device, or location tracking
- Private communications
- Persistent personal sentiment profiles

## Measures

\[
SAI_t = w_L L_t + w_C C_t + w_A A_t + w_D D_t
\]

\[
F_t = 1 - \max(
r_{\text{telemetry-required}},
r_{\text{connectivity-required}},
r_{\text{certificate-required}},
r_{\text{accessibility-barrier}},
r_{\text{appeal-barrier}},
r_{\text{privacy-burden}}
)
\]

## Required Safeguards

- Aggregate reporting only
- Public methodology
- Minimum-group-size protection
- Accessible participation channels
- Human review
- Correction and appeal pathway
- Data-minimization and retention limits
- No use for eligibility, enforcement, surveillance, or social scoring
