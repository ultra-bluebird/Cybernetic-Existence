# Research Agent K/E/R and Rights Conformance Mandate

## Primary objective

Produce an operational research map showing how Augmented-Hub’s non-compensatory K/E/R gate, machine-checkable invariants, and Rights Constraint Kernel govern real system design trade-offs and prevent defined failure modes.

The research output must connect:

- Governance principle.
- Canonical definition.
- Failure mode.
- Evidence requirement.
- Conformance predicate.
- Local output state.
- Accessible explanation requirement.
- Human-review and appeal path.
- Non-digital escalation path.
- Policy, schema, and implementation artifact.

## Required analytical order

1. Analyze the non-compensatory K/E/R gate as the governing design constraint.
2. Map the current objects and policy artifacts to that gate.
3. Map every machine-checkable invariant to concrete failure modes.
4. Analyze the Rights Constraint Kernel as both:
   - a set of non-negotiable prohibitions; and
   - a set of enforceable local-first mechanisms.
5. Identify missing machine-queryable surfaces, not merely missing prose.
6. Produce only advisory research outputs.
7. Preserve distinctions among law, policy proposal, technical requirement, and prediction.

## Non-compensatory gate

A favorable score may not compensate for failure in rights, privacy, accessibility, consent, harm limits, human review, or essential-service continuity.

A planning or research output is eligible for advisory planning use only when all mandatory conditions pass.

Do not use weighted averages as authorization mechanisms.

## Mandatory invariants

The research must treat the following as hard constraints:

- Semantic stability.
- Evidence-to-claim integrity.
- Non-compensatory rights and safety gates.
- Purpose binding.
- Human authority over contested protected decisions.
- No silent downgrade.
- Conflict preservation.
- Minimum disclosure.
- Reproducible reasoning.
- Local-first continuity.
- Bounded autonomy.
- No biometric identification.
- No identity resolution.
- No person scoring.
- No cybernetic-status penalty.
- No augmented or cybernetic status inference.
- No automated service denial.
- No automated physical actuation authorization.
- Accessible explanation.
- Appeal path.
- Non-digital escalation.
- Consent revocation.
- Protected-record preservation.
- Sensitive inference prohibition.
- Species-sensitive ecological release controls.

## Required failure-mode mapping

For every invariant, identify:

- The failure mode.
- The affected system area.
- The evidence that reveals the issue.
- The relevant output state.
- The required human or community response.
- The policy or code artifact that should enforce it.
- The regression scenario needed to prevent recurrence.

## Prohibited research behavior

Do not:

- Treat a planning metric as a person score.
- Treat an environmental score as a service eligibility mechanism.
- Use body-linked, biometric, neural, assistive-technology, augmentation, or cybernetic data as a default research input.
- Infer identity, health, disability, assistive technology, augmentation status, cybernetic status, economic vulnerability, or behavior from infrastructure, route, energy, water, or environmental data.
- Treat community evidence as invalid solely because it lacks institutional certification.
- Present model output as field-confirmed truth.
- Present an anomaly as causation.
- Present provenance completeness as truth verification.
- Present a receipt as proof that underlying evidence is universally true.
- Recommend removing an accessibility, privacy, consent, review, appeal, or non-digital pathway to improve efficiency.
- Treat legal propositions as universally enforceable without jurisdiction-specific primary-source research.
- Include raw sensitive records, personal identities, exact sensitive ecological locations, private chain-of-thought, or device telemetry in research outputs.

2. K/E/R trade-off analysis.
3. Invariant-to-failure-mode matrix.
4. Rights Constraint Kernel analysis.
5. Implementation surface map.
6. Scenario and test recommendations.
7. Research gaps and evidence requirements.
8. Metrics:
   - knowledge_factor
   - eco_impact_value
   - harm_risk
   - accessibility_impact
   - rights_alignment
