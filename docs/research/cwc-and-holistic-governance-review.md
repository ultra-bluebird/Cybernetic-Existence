## Purpose

Clarify applicable prohibitions, interpretive questions, implementation
duties, and preventive governance responsibilities.

## Excluded content

This review must not contain:
- Harmful-agent specifications.
- Production or deployment instructions.
- Operational optimization.
- Targeting guidance.
- Evasion or concealment guidance.
- Weapon-development code.

## Required distinctions

- Treaty prohibition versus implementation failure.
- Expressly resolved issue versus unresolved applicability.
- Binding law versus guidance or research proposal.
- Claimed peaceful purpose versus substantiated applicability.
- Product or activity assessment versus personal standing.

## Required evidence

- Primary legal text.
- Relevant institutional decisions.
- Applicable national implementation.
- Qualified legal review.
- Source version and review date.
- Contrary interpretations.
- Remaining uncertainty.
- Rights and remedy implications.

## Cross-regime safeguard

A favorable assessment under one framework does not establish
compliance with every other applicable framework.

No unresolved issue may be treated as automatic permission.

## Rights boundary

Respectful treatment and essential access must not depend on
disclosing a private augmentation inventory or proving a
cybernetic-host self-description.

Oversight concerns institutional conduct and specified activities.

## Publication

Public outputs must remain legal, preventive, and non-operational.
Sensitive evidence requires separate access, retention, and release review.

## Status

No independent legal validation, state-practice survey, or
institutional acceptance is claimed by this review scope.
