# Augmented Citizen Rights Research Map

## Status

Research and technical-policy planning document.

This document is not legal advice and does not determine legal status, disability status, institutional status, device status, or the outcome of any complaint, accommodation request, or court matter.

## Purpose

Map existing legal and standards-based protections that can support privacy, accessible communication, equal public-service access, data minimization, and continuity of host-reported terminology.

## Functional-needs principle

A person may request an accessible service adjustment using a functional description of the barrier and needed adjustment.

A request for:

- shade-aware routing
- lower-heat traversal
- step-free access
- curb-ramp information
- rest-location information
- predictable communication
- plain-language explanation
- human assistance
- accessible digital content
- privacy-preserving service access

does not require disclosure of unrelated private terminology, private records, health details, identity details, or system descriptions.

## United States

### ADA Title II

Potential relevance:

- State and local government services, programs, activities, websites, mobile applications, transit information, and public communication.
- Reasonable modifications to policies, practices, or procedures when necessary to avoid disability discrimination.
- Effective communication and appropriate auxiliary aids and services.

Research questions:

1. Which public entity operates the service?
2. What specific barrier prevents equal access?
3. What modification, auxiliary aid, route feature, or human fallback is requested?
4. Can the request be fulfilled without diagnosis disclosure?
5. Is there a written accessibility coordinator, grievance procedure, or complaint path?

### ADA Title III

Potential relevance:

- Public accommodations operated by private entities.
- Effective communication and accessible goods, services, facilities, privileges, advantages, and accommodations.

Research questions:

1. Is the service a covered public accommodation?
2. What communication or access barrier exists?
3. What reasonable modification or auxiliary aid is requested?
4. What equivalent access path is offered?

### Section 504 and Section 508

Potential relevance:

- Federally funded programs and federal information and communications technology.
- Digital accessibility, accessible information, and nondiscriminatory program access.

Research questions:

1. Does the program receive federal financial assistance?
2. Is the service federal information technology or federally procured technology?
3. What accessibility standard or procurement requirement applies?

## European Union

### GDPR

Potential relevance:

- Personal data processing, including accessibility profiles, location data, health data, biometric data, identifiers, and inferred attributes.
- Purpose limitation, data minimization, storage limitation, data accuracy, security, privacy by design, and privacy by default.
- Rights of access, correction, deletion where applicable, restriction, and objection.

Research questions:

1. What personal data is being processed?
2. What is the lawful basis for processing?
3. Is special-category data involved?
4. Can the purpose be met with anonymized, pseudonymized, or short-lived data?
5. What retention period and deletion process apply?
6. Can the service use a selective-disclosure presentation rather than a full profile?

## Digital identity standards

### W3C DID Core

Potential relevance:

- Persistent decentralized identifiers.
- Verification methods.
- Service endpoints.
- Key rotation and document updates.

### W3C Verifiable Credentials

Potential relevance:

- Purpose-specific claims.
- Holder-controlled presentation.
- Selective disclosure.
- Machine-verifiable integrity.

Research questions:

1. What is the minimum claim required for a service?
2. Can the claim be short-lived and non-correlatable?
3. Can a holder disclose only a route-accessibility entitlement rather than an identity profile?
4. Can the verifier avoid retaining the presentation after completion?

## Recommended advocacy format

Use a short request containing:

1. The service or public-space barrier.
2. The functional access need.
3. The requested change.
4. The preferred communication method.
5. A request for minimum necessary data handling.
6. A request for written explanation and appeal or correction path.

Avoid making the request depend on public recognition of a private designation.
