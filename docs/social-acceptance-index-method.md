# Social Acceptance Index Method

## Purpose

The Social Acceptance Index measures inclusion conditions in a defined public,
civic, organizational, or service context. It does not measure acceptance of people.

The index supports service improvement, accessibility review, safeguard review,
and governance transparency.

## Unit of Analysis

The unit of analysis is one of:

- A service workflow
- A public information channel
- A civic participation pathway
- A defined organizational policy context
- An aggregated public-discourse corpus with documented provenance

The unit of analysis is never:

- A person
- A household
- A identity group
- A neighborhood population
- A service user profile
- A contributor reputation record

## Required Reporting Conditions

An index result may be reported only when:

- Evidence provenance is complete.
- A documented aggregation threshold is met.
- No person-level data are used.
- No identity, demographic, medical, neural, biometric, disability,
  communication, or augmentation status is inferred.
- No private messages are processed.
- No result can trigger an automated or adverse external action.
- A correction route and review owner are documented.
- A retention expiration date is assigned.

## Index Dimensions

```text
Access equivalence:
Can a person complete core service workflows without certification,
identity disclosure, or inaccessible interaction requirements?

Participation availability:
Can people participate through accessible, low-disclosure, non-retaliatory
feedback and civic engagement routes?

Safeguard coverage:
Are human fallback, correction, appeal, and harm-reporting paths available
and usable?

Representation quality:
Do published materials meet dignity, accessibility, and non-stigmatizing
communication criteria?

Voluntary feedback signal:
Do thresholded, optional, privacy-preserving feedback results identify
service conditions that need improvement?

Governance maturity:
Are data limits, retention, review cadence, material-change notices,
and accountability paths documented?
```

## Not a Legal Determination

Results do not establish legal compliance, legal liability, legal protected status,
or an individual right to a particular outcome. Jurisdiction-specific legal review,
where needed, remains a deployment-layer activity.

## No Person Scoring

The system must not use any index input or output for:

- Identity validation
- Identity certification
- Eligibility
- Access prioritization
- Hiring
- Housing
- Benefits
- Insurance
- Fraud scoring
- Advertising
- Surveillance
- Law enforcement
- Reputation scoring
- Individual behavioral prediction
