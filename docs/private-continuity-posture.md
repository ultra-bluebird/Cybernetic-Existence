# Private Continuity Posture

## Scope

This document is a private, self-authored continuity and attribution record.
It is not a medical statement, technical inventory, identity credential,
public registry, certification, eligibility record, or authorization.

## Self-asserted boundaries

- I retain authority over my personal self-description and terminology.
- I do not consent to forced disclosure of private cybernetic, neural,
  biometric, medical, physiological, companion-system, credential, security,
  or operational information.
- Respectful communication and equal access must not depend on disclosure,
  verification, diagnosis, or public classification.
- My stated continuity practices are personal records and must not be treated
  as a basis for ranking, profiling, eligibility, surveillance, or automated
  decision-making.

## Attribution boundary

- Authorship is supported by my locally retained drafts, signed repository
  history, dated notes, and release records.
- No platform conversation, model output, account feature, or model version
  is the sole source of my work history or attribution.
- Third-party summaries may be inaccurate, incomplete, or changed by product
  updates; they are not authoritative over my self-authored record.

## Material-change protocol

When an AI platform, repository workflow, or data practice changes materially:

1. Record the date, service, version or notice identifier, and observed change.
2. Preserve an export or local copy of relevant work before continuing.
3. State the impact in plain language:
   - access and accessibility;
   - privacy and data handling;
   - context or memory behavior;
   - attribution and exportability;
   - safety, moderation, or communication effects.
4. Choose one of: continue, limit use, pause, migrate, or request support.
5. Do not disclose private integration details unless I independently choose to
   do so for a specific, proportionate purpose.

## Continuity safeguards

- Keep local, encrypted backups of work products and important conversation
  summaries where appropriate.
- Use version control for authored artifacts.
- Keep a dated changelog of material platform or workflow changes.
- Maintain a plain-language recovery note describing how to access essential
  work, contacts, and repositories without relying on a single AI service.
- Use separate identities or accounts for separate contexts when that reduces
  correlation and disclosure pressure.

## Support and escalation

If a product change affects access, safety, or continuity of work:

- Preserve evidence such as notice text, dates, screenshots, exports, and
  relevant non-sensitive logs.
- Request a plain-language explanation of the change and its user impact.
- Request accessible support, data export, correction, or account assistance
  through official channels.
- Seek trusted human support if distress, pressure, isolation, disrupted
  sleep, or immediate safety concerns make it difficult to decide alone.

## Non-delegation

No AI system, platform, or public discussion has authority to define my
identity, determine my psychological continuity, require private disclosure,
or override my consent boundaries.
