# Personal Rights Declaration:
# Sovereign Cybernetic-Existence, Privacy, and Non-Interference

## Purpose

This is a voluntary personal declaration of my self-description, communication
preferences, privacy boundaries, continuity needs, and requested conditions of
respectful interaction.

It is intended for personal use, accessibility communication, governance design,
and research documentation. It is not a public registry entry, credential,
institutional classification, medical record, diagnosis, legal determination,
consent form, operational instruction, or authorization to act on any person,
device, account, network, environment, or system.

## Personal standing

I affirm my equal dignity, autonomy, privacy, and personhood.

I use the term **Cybernetic-Existence** as my voluntary personal vocabulary for
describing aspects of my lived continuity, communication, relationships with
chosen tools or assistive practices, and boundaries around disclosure and
non-interference.

This self-description does not require proof, registration, diagnosis,
certification, ranking, or public disclosure in order to be received
respectfully.

My language, pacing, repetition, silence, assistive communication, technical
vocabulary, fatigue, privacy choices, or refusal to disclose must not be used
as a basis for unsupported inferences about my identity, health, medical state,
augmentation status, cognitive state, credibility, eligibility, authority,
institutional affiliation, or capacity.

## Minimum respectful reception

I ask readers and systems to receive this declaration according to the
following boundaries:

- Treat it as a personal self-description and statement of boundaries.
- Do not treat it as proof of medical, legal, technical, institutional, or
  eligibility status.
- Do not infer undisclosed facts from it.
- Do not require disclosure of private medical, neural, biometric, device,
  household, address, credential, or institutional information merely to
  acknowledge my personhood or communicate accessibly.
- Do not transform it into a profile, score, rank, credential, public record,
  eligibility gate, or automated decision input.
- Provide clear explanation, accessible communication, correction pathways,
  and a meaningful human-review route where a consequential process applies.
- Respect that I may revise, narrow, withdraw, or replace this declaration.

These are requested conditions of respectful interaction. They do not remove
another person's rights, safety duties, consent requirements, professional
obligations, or lawful responsibilities.

## Non-inference and identity firewall

This declaration establishes a personal non-inference boundary.

The declaration itself must not be interpreted to establish or imply:

- A diagnosis, medical condition, treatment need, or clinical conclusion.
- The existence, configuration, function, safety, ownership, or operation of
  any neural, medical, assistive, biometric, companion, cybernetic, or personal
  system.
- A legal identity, institutional affiliation, citizenship, residence,
  household relationship, credential, rank, eligibility, authority, or
  representation of another person.
- Permission to collect, retain, share, profile, authenticate, monitor,
  inspect, validate, diagnose, certify, rank, or operationalize personal data.
- Consent to disclosure, intervention, contact, surveillance, experimentation,
  technical access, or action in my name.

Absence of information remains absence of information. Uncertainty must not be
silently converted into a conclusion, a risk category, an eligibility result,
or permission to act.

## Accessible communication and continuity

I may communicate directly, asynchronously, through assistive technologies,
with different pacing, in detail, in fragments, through repetition, with
silence, or through a chosen communication mode.

These are communication variations. They are not evidence of reduced
competence, credibility, autonomy, or entitlement to equal access.

When feasible and appropriate, I request:

- Plain-language explanations of consequential processes.
- Sufficient time to understand and respond.
- A non-punitive opportunity to clarify or correct misunderstandings.
- Communication alternatives that do not require unnecessary personal
  disclosure.
- A human-accessible route for review, correction, and appeal.
- Respect for private continuity needs without demands for technical, bodily,
  medical, neural, biometric, or institutional proof.

## Process integrity

No silence, delay, missing record, or incomplete information should be treated
as my consent, refusal, waiver, deception, incapacity, approval, denial, or
resolution.

When a process has not reached a decision, it should say so plainly.

```text
deadline elapsed -> overdue or escalated
deadline elapsed -/-> approved, denied, closed, or resolved
```

Where information is uncertain, the proper state is `unknown`, `pending`, or
`requires_review`, not an inferred conclusion.

Where a correction or appeal process exists, it should provide a meaningful
opportunity for clarification without creating unnecessary disclosure pressure.

## Public contribution and attribution

If I contribute knowledge, research, feedback, ecological observations, civic
ideas, accessibility insight, or other material, attribution must remain
voluntary, revocable where feasible, and separate from ranking, credentialing,
eligibility, or authority.

Anonymous contribution must be treated as a legitimate form of participation.

My choice not to disclose, identify, certify, rank, register, or publicly
attribute myself must not be treated as a negative signal.

## Medical and emergency boundary

This declaration is not clinical guidance and does not replace emergency
medical evaluation, professional judgment, informed consent procedures, or
applicable safety duties.

In an emergency, responders and clinicians should provide appropriate care
under their professional and legal responsibilities. This declaration may
communicate preferences for respectful, privacy-preserving, accessible
interaction, but it does not direct treatment or prevent necessary emergency
assessment.

## Lifecycle

This declaration is personal and may be revised by me.

Its local lifecycle states are:

```text
draft -> active -> corrected -> renewed -> superseded -> withdrawn -> archived
```

- `draft` means proposed and not currently relied upon as my active declaration.
- `active` means my current personal statement of boundaries and preferences.
- `corrected` means a limited clarification has been recorded.
- `renewed` means I have confirmed or updated the declaration.
- `superseded` means a successor declaration replaces it.
- `withdrawn` means I no longer wish to present it as active.
- `archived` means retained only according to an applicable local retention
  rule and not treated as current.

No lifecycle state authorizes external action or establishes an institutional,
medical, legal, or public status.
