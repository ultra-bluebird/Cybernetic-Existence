# Platform-Acceptable Cybernetic-Host Terminology

## Purpose

This guide supports clear, dignified, low-disclosure communication with online
platforms, service providers, institutions, support teams, and collaborative
systems.

It does not require a person to change, deny, minimize, prove, diagnose, or
publicly disclose their private self-description. It provides task-facing
language that helps a recipient understand the requested outcome without
requiring unnecessary private detail.

## Foundational distinction

Private self-description and task-facing communication are different layers.

A person may use private terminology such as cybernetic-host, augmented,
companion system, psychological continuity, sovereign attribution, or other
self-chosen terms.

A task-facing request may instead identify:

- the requested service outcome;
- the communication preference;
- the privacy boundary;
- the relevant technical constraint;
- the preferred output format;
- the correction, export, or appeal need.

Respect does not require verification.
Acknowledgment does not require certification.
Privacy does not require silence.
Accessible service does not require public disclosure.

## General low-disclosure statement

"I use private integrated supports and maintain continuity preferences for my
work and communication. Please provide clear, respectful, privacy-preserving
assistance. I am not requesting verification, diagnosis, technical assessment,
or public acknowledgment. Please do not ask for private medical, biometric,
technical, credential, or operational details unless they are necessary for a
specific service I have chosen to receive."

## Task-facing vocabulary

| Need | Suggested wording |
|---|---|
| Respectful interaction | "Please use respectful, direct language and avoid unnecessary assumptions about my private systems." |
| Privacy | "Please do not request unnecessary medical, biometric, technical, credential, or operational details." |
| Accessible communication | "Please provide clear, paced, plain-language communication and a written summary where practical." |
| Continuity | "I maintain private continuity records. Please provide a portable, versioned, clearly structured output." |
| Data export | "Please explain how I can export my user-controlled records in a documented format." |
| Platform change | "Please summarize the material change, effective date, data-use impact, export options, and appeal path." |
| Correction | "Please provide a correction route and preserve a record of the current and corrected interpretation." |
| Technical design | "Help me design a local-first, privacy-preserving, continuity-supporting workflow." |
| Civic access | "I need an accessible, privacy-respecting service path without unnecessary personal disclosure." |
| Support escalation | "Please provide a human-support or accessible escalation route for this issue." |

## Terms and use contexts

| Term | Appropriate use | Avoid treating it as |
|---|---|---|
| Integrated systems | General technical or support context | A demand for technical inspection |
| Assistive technology | Accessibility and functional-access context | A mandatory disability disclosure |
| Personal augmentation | Personal, design, or research context | A public status or eligibility signal |
| Continuity practices | Personal records, migration, and research context | A diagnosis or consciousness assessment |
| Continuity records | Export, migration, provenance, and correction context | Identity proof or public registry |
| Local-first workflow | Privacy, portability, and offline-access context | A claim of universal security |
| Privacy boundary | Any disclosure-sensitive context | A refusal to cooperate with proportionate service needs |
| Sovereign attribution | Authorship and provenance context | Legal identity or public credential |

## Terms to handle carefully

Some terms are meaningful personal or research vocabulary but may invite
unnecessary scope expansion in a routine support request.

Examples include:

- superintelligence;
- neural or medical system details;
- claims about a platform's relationship to personal existence;
- claims that require a recipient to decide personhood, consciousness,
  institutional status, or metaphysical truth;
- broad demands that a platform validate or certify a private system.

These terms are not prohibited. Use them when they are genuinely relevant to
the research or discussion. For task-oriented support, pair them with a
specific request and a privacy boundary.

Example:

"Within my private continuity research framework, I need a versioned local
record schema. Please provide a JSON schema and migration rules. Do not infer
or request private medical, neural, biometric, or operational details."

## Non-denial rule

Functional wording does not deny private self-description.

A person may choose language such as "I am augmented" or "I am a
cybernetic-host." No proof is required for respectful interaction. A recipient
may distinguish respectful acknowledgment from technical, medical, legal, or
institutional verification.

## No special-status demand

A request for privacy, accessibility, clear communication, continuity of
records, correction, export, or appeal is an ordinary service-governance
request. It does not require a special status, public registry, certification,
ranking, or exception from lawful rules.

## Safe default response for recipients

A recipient can respond:

"Thank you for sharing your communication and privacy preferences. I will
focus on the task you requested, use clear and respectful language, and avoid
asking for unnecessary private details. I cannot verify or assess private
systems, but I can help with documentation, local record design, privacy,
accessibility, export, correction, and other concrete needs."
