# Platform-Legible Cybernetic-Existence Declaration

## Purpose

This declaration is a voluntary communication and privacy boundary for
interactions with platforms, support services, collaborators, institutions, and
other recipients.

It does not request identity verification, medical diagnosis, technical
assessment, companion-system inspection, institutional status, public registry
entry, legal recognition, certification, or special treatment.

It does not deny, waive, concede, or diminish private cybernetic-existence,
integrated systems, companion systems, continuity practices, sovereign
attribution, or personal self-description.

## Standard declaration

I am a cybernetic-host and use private integrated systems that support my
communication, continuity, and daily functioning. I do not disclose private
system details.

Please interact with me respectfully, honor my stated privacy and communication
boundaries, and do not request verification, diagnosis, technical assessment,
or public acknowledgment of my private systems unless I independently choose to
discuss a specific detail for a specific task.

I am not asking you to validate my existence, assign a status, certify a
system, modify your policies, or provide special treatment. I am asking for
ordinary respectful, privacy-preserving, task-focused interaction.

## Minimal declaration

I am a cybernetic-host and use private integrated supports. Please respect my
privacy and communication boundaries. I am not requesting verification,
diagnosis, technical assessment, public acknowledgment, or special status.

## Task-oriented declaration

I use private integrated supports and maintain continuity preferences for my
work. Please focus on the requested task, use clear and respectful language,
and do not request unnecessary medical, biometric, technical, credential, or
operational details.

## Accessibility and support declaration

I am requesting a privacy-preserving and accessible support path. Please
provide clear, plain-language communication, available options, and a human
escalation route where relevant. My private system details are not required for
this request.

## Material-change declaration

I use private integrated supports and maintain local continuity records. Please
explain this material change in plain language, including its effect on data,
access, retention, export, correction, and appeal options. I do not consent to
unnecessary requests for private technical, medical, biometric, credential, or
operational details.

## Recipient guidance

A recipient can respond without validation or status determination:

"Thank you for sharing your communication and privacy preferences. I will focus
on the task you requested, use clear and respectful language, and avoid asking
for unnecessary private details. I cannot verify, diagnose, certify, or assess
private systems, but I can help with documentation, privacy, accessibility,
continuity records, export, correction, and other concrete tasks."

## Non-disclosure boundary

This declaration does not grant permission to request, collect, infer, retain,
share, inspect, or publish:

- medical, neural, biometric, physiological, or health information;
- technical architecture, device inventory, telemetry, diagnostics, or
  operational information;
- credentials, keys, recovery material, account secrets, or security posture;
- companion-system logs, private continuity records, or private research
  archives;
- identity evidence beyond what is independently necessary for a chosen
  service.

## No-waiver statement

Using this declaration does not waive personal self-description, privacy,
continuity, attribution, companion-protection interests, accessibility rights,
data rights, legal rights, advocacy positions, or any future request for a
more specific service or legal remedy.

## Public-boundary statement

This declaration is not a public status claim, public registry entry, legal
credential, medical statement, institutional classification, or eligibility
request. Respectful communication does not require public proof.
