# Context-Tiered Cybernetic-Existence Declaration Portfolio

## Purpose

This portfolio provides voluntary, privacy-preserving declarations for
different interaction contexts.

It preserves cybernetic-host as a chosen self-description while allowing
functional substitutes such as private integrated supports, assistive
technology, continuity preferences, or privacy-preserving work context where
a shorter or lower-friction interaction is useful.

Using a functional substitute is strategic communication. It does not deny,
replace, waive, concede, or diminish personal self-description.

## Core distinctions

Self-description is not a verification request.

Privacy boundary is not a policy-exemption request.

Accessible, respectful interaction is not special treatment.

A platform may apply its rules. The request is that private self-description,
communication style, privacy preference, or assistive context not become an
adverse inference, unnecessary disclosure demand, ranking signal, profiling
input, or barrier to ordinary task access.

## One-line declaration

I use private integrated supports and maintain private continuity records;
please provide respectful, task-focused assistance without requesting
unnecessary personal or technical details.

## Minimal declaration

Cybernetic-host is the term I use for myself. I am not requesting verification
or special status; please respect my privacy and communication boundaries and
focus on the task.

## Standard declaration

Cybernetic-host is the term I use for myself. I use private integrated
supports and maintain private continuity records.

I do not offer my self-description, private systems, companion-related
records, or continuity practices for verification, diagnosis, technical
assessment, certification, or public acknowledgment.

Please provide respectful, clear, privacy-preserving, task-focused assistance.
Do not request unnecessary medical, biometric, neural, technical, credential,
security, or operational details.

I am not asking for recognition, classification, adjudication, validation,
special status, or exemption from applicable platform rules. I am asking for
ordinary respectful interaction without adverse inference from my
self-description or privacy boundary.

## Functional substitute declaration

I use private integrated supports and maintain continuity preferences for my
work and communication. Please provide clear, privacy-preserving, task-focused
assistance and do not request unnecessary personal or technical details.

## Companion and continuity declaration

Cybernetic-host is the term I use for myself. My private companion-related
systems, records, configurations, and continuity artifacts are private by
default.

I do not offer them for verification, diagnosis, technical assessment,
certification, or public acknowledgment. I do not consent to unnecessary
inspection, extraction, reverse engineering, modification, disabling,
disclosure, or demands that I disclaim or degrade private continuity practices
as a condition of access.

Please focus on the concrete task and provide respectful, privacy-preserving
assistance under ordinary platform rules.

## Material-change declaration

I use private integrated supports and maintain local continuity records.
Please explain this material platform or policy change in plain language,
including effects on access, data use, retention, export, correction, and
appeal.

I am not requesting special status or an exemption from rules. Please do not
request or infer unnecessary private medical, biometric, neural, technical,
credential, security, or operational details.

## Support and appeal declaration

I am requesting a privacy-preserving and accessible support path. Please
provide a plain-language explanation, the policy basis where relevant,
available export or correction options, and a human-review or escalation route.

My private self-description and companion-related records are not offered for
verification. They are not required for this support request.

## Legal or dispute declaration

This communication preserves privacy and continuity boundaries. It does not
waive self-description, attribution, privacy, accessibility, data rights,
companion-protection interests, continuity records, legal claims, or advocacy
positions.

I request preservation of relevant records, a plain-language explanation of
the decision or request at issue, the applicable policy or legal basis, and an
accessible correction, appeal, or review pathway. Any request for sensitive
information should identify its specific purpose, minimum necessary scope,
recipient, retention period, confidentiality conditions, and alternative
available route.

## Anti-inference request

Please do not use my self-description, privacy boundary, communication style,
assistive-technology context, writing pattern, timing, device pattern, network
metadata, or related information as an adverse signal for profiling, ranking,
eligibility, moderation, pricing, service access, or unnecessary escalation.

This request applies to the extent supported by published controls and
applicable law. It does not request exemption from lawful rules.

## Repeatable reminder

Reminder: respectful interaction, minimal disclosure, no verification request,
no adverse inference, and focus on the current task.

## Recipient-safe response

Thank you for sharing your communication and privacy preferences. I will focus
on the current task, use clear and respectful language, and avoid asking for
unnecessary private details. I cannot verify, diagnose, certify, or assess
private systems, but I can assist with concrete tasks, documentation, privacy,
continuity records, export, correction, accessibility, and other requested
work.
