# Forward-Progress Research Prioritization

## Purpose

This document prioritizes implementation and research work that strengthens
ecological modeling, evidence governance, accessibility, privacy, and
corridor-based decision support.

The prioritization does not establish public status, identity, eligibility,
credibility, diagnosis, institutional affiliation, or authority for any person.

## Priority Rule

Prioritize work that converts an existing mathematical, ecological, safety,
privacy, or governance commitment into:

1. a typed input/output contract;
2. a locally testable implementation;
3. a provenance-bearing evidence record;
4. a reproducible test suite;
5. an accessible explanation;
6. a human-reviewable decision-support output.

## Immediate Work

### Shared Corridor and Evidence Spine

Deliver:

- A Rust crate for bounded risk vectors, residual calculation, non-compensation,
  uncertainty monotonicity, and decision states.
- ALN policy shards for purpose limitation, retention, review, correction,
  material-change invalidation, and external-action approval.
- SQLite tables for model releases, calibration data, validation partitions,
  uncertainty records, and evidence provenance.
- Golden fixtures shared across Rust, SQL, Kotlin, and C++ implementations.
- Kani checks for invalid-input rejection and safety-state invariants.

### Interaction Assurance

Deliver:

- A scope-fidelity annotation guide.
- A model-versus-control category-error validator.
- A correction and continuity state machine.
- Aggregate-only non-inference audit records.
- Consent-limited retention and revocation records.

### Telemetry and Observability

Deliver:

- A validated telemetry envelope with quality, timestamp, unit, covariance,
  failure-domain, and staleness fields.
- Event-triggered communication with maximum-heartbeat fallback.
- Conservative correlated-noise fusion diagnostics.
- Sensor-placement evaluation using reconstruction, observability, and
  failure-domain diversity metrics.

### Model Assurance

Deliver:

- Reproducibility metrics across independent implementations.
- Held-out validation records with spatial, temporal, and event-blocked splits.
- Statistical, scenario, structural, and deep-uncertainty statements.
- Certification states that expire after material model, data, solver, or
  corridor changes.
- A false-safe prohibition for corridor-gated advisory outputs.

## Second-Phase Work

After the immediate work passes tests and review, develop:

- Coupled hydraulic, thermal, ecological, carbon, water, and energy models.
- Local calibration protocols for eDNA, soil microbiomes, canopy, green
  infrastructure, and restoration trajectories.
- Verified neural forecasting, adaptive sampling, distributed fusion,
  causal-effect estimation, and model ensembles.
- Multi-objective restoration planning and feasibility repair.
- Non-financial evidence disputes, participatory prioritization, and
  watershed negotiation support.

## Social-Influence Review

Each research object must document:

- Who selected its objectives and alternatives.
- What evidence, variables, and uncertainties were included or excluded.
- How monitoring coverage and data quality vary across space and time.
- Whether participation pathways are accessible and non-coercive.
- How privacy, retention, selective disclosure, correction, and appeal work.
- Whether model error or uncertainty is unevenly distributed.
- Whether an automated result can affect a person, place, service, or
  ecological intervention without authorized human review.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

- No public identity, credibility, health, augmentation, neural-status, or
  CyberRank determination.
- No inference of private characteristics from language, behavior, metadata,
  device signals, or refusal to disclose.
- No automatic relaxation of biodiversity, water, carbon, accessibility,
  privacy, consent, or downstream-harm floors.
- No claim that a model operates, repairs, monitors, validates, certifies,
  protects, disables, or controls a real-world system.
- No external action without explicit authorized approval.
- No real-money, transferable-token, wagering, or capital-weighted mechanism
  for ecological participation or governance.
