# Trusted Spine Architecture

## Purpose

The trusted spine converts mathematical, ecological, privacy, accessibility,
and governance commitments into reproducible executable contracts.

It supports ecological and civic decision assistance. It does not establish
identity, credibility, eligibility, health, neural status, augmentation status,
institutional affiliation, public rank, or authority for any person.

## Contract Chain

Every decision-support output must trace to:

1. a formal specification;
2. an executable contract;
3. a provenance-bearing evidence record;
4. verification and validation evidence;
5. an accessible governance explanation.

An output without a complete trace is exploratory only.

## Decision States

- EXPLORATORY
- CALIBRATED
- VALIDATED_ADVISORY
- CORRIDOR_GATED
- INDETERMINATE
- NOT_CERTIFIABLE
- EXPIRED

## Immediate Priorities

1. Interaction integrity:
   scope persistence, model-control separation, non-inference, correction
   handling, and continuity preservation.

2. Evidence and assurance:
   provenance, consent scope, retention, reproducibility, verification,
   calibration, held-out validation, uncertainty, and material-change review.

3. Shared corridor kernel:
   bounded risk vectors, residual evaluation, non-compensation, uncertainty
   monotonicity, and conservative decision states.

4. Telemetry assurance:
   quality validation, observability, staleness, failure-domain diversity,
   correlated-noise handling, and heartbeat fallback.

5. Coupled ecological research:
   only after local data, documented calibration, and validation are available.

## Non-Compensation

Non-negotiable protections cannot be offset by benefits in another category.

A decision is review-required when any non-offsettable condition fails,
regardless of aggregate score.

## Interaction Integrity

A model request must not be converted into a claim of real-world operation,
monitoring, repair, calibration, control, protection, certification, or
authorization.

Self-description and communication variation must not alter credibility,
technical depth, factuality standards, caveat density, service access, or
eligibility.

Explicit accessibility preferences may alter presentation only.

## Social-Influence Review

Every material artifact must document:

- agenda-setting choices and excluded alternatives;
- measurement coverage and missingness;
- model weights, priors, and omitted variables;
- participation accessibility and concentration risk;
- privacy exposure, retention, and selective-disclosure boundaries;
- automation-bias safeguards;
- corrective and re-clarification burden;
- uncertainty and human-review pathways.

## Ecological Decision Support

Ecological outputs are decision support only.

A corridor-gated advisory requires:

- verified implementation;
- numerical solution verification;
- locally documented calibration;
- held-out validation;
- uncertainty coverage;
- domain-of-applicability pass;
- reproducible test evidence;
- zero false-safe outcomes on the declared validation suite;
- accessible review and correction routes.
