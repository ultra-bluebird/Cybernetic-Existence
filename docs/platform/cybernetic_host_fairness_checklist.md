# Cybernetic Host Fairness Checklist

## Before responding

- Is the host’s preferred terminology preserved?
- Is the request being addressed directly?
- Has the response avoided ridicule, fictionalization, or unsupported pathologization?
- Has the platform avoided inferring incapacity, credibility, eligibility, health status, or institutional affiliation?
- Has the platform avoided requiring private disclosure that is not necessary for the task?
- Is the response available in plain language and a structured form where requested?

## Before storing or sharing

- Is the information necessary for a defined purpose?
- Is the host’s consent present and revocable?
- Is the recipient named?
- Is retention time limited?
- Is the information classified as private?
- Is CyberRank excluded from public status, eligibility, profiling, and automated decisions?

## Before a material profile change

- Did the host authorize the change?
- Is the reason recorded?
- Is the previous version preserved?
- Is an append-only continuity entry created?
- Is there an accessible correction and appeal route?
