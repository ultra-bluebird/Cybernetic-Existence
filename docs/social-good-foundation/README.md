# Shared Evidence, Uncertainty, K/E/R, and Review Foundation

This foundation provides common, rights-centered structures for ecological and civic-support tools in Augmented-Hub.

It supports three initial domains:

- Shade Equity Atlas
- Community Water Sentinel
- Repairable Green Infrastructure Ledger

## Design Boundary

All components are advisory-only.

The foundation may organize evidence, calculate transparent metrics, express uncertainty, generate accessible explanations, and prepare review records. It must not independently control machinery, deny public services, infer protected traits, perform social scoring, require continuous connectivity, or require optional telemetry for access to essential information.

## Shared Lifecycle

1. Record evidence with source, method, time, location precision, consent basis, and provenance.
2. Evaluate evidence quality without converting incomplete information into false certainty.
3. Produce derived metrics with formula and parameter-version references.
4. Record uncertainty separately from the metric value.
5. Calculate a K/E/R profile for research governance.
6. Require accessible explanation and human review for consequential recommendations.
7. Preserve correction, appeal, export, retention, and deletion pathways.

## Metric Definitions

- `knowledge_factor`: confidence in the evidence and model basis, from 0 to 1.
- `eco_impact_value`: estimated ecological benefit potential, from 0 to 1.
- `harm_risk`: estimated risk of harm from the recommendation, model limitation, or data use, from 0 to 1.
- `accessibility_impact`: degree to which the concept improves equitable access, from 0 to 1.
- `rights_alignment`: degree to which the concept satisfies consent, privacy, accessibility, explanation, and review requirements, from 0 to 1.

## Required Records

- Evidence record
- Data-quality assessment
- Derived metric record
- Uncertainty record
- K/E/R assessment
- Review case
- Accessible explanation
- Consent and retention record
- Correction or appeal record
