## Required registry content

Each label entry must specify:
- Exact phrase and normalized study term.
- Jurisdiction or standards framework.
- Product or certification scope.
- Applicable pathway or assessment route.
- Source and source version.
- Meaning of the claim.
- Claims not established.
- Verification method.
- Review date.
- Unresolved applicability questions.

A generic "certified" entry is insufficient without its framework and scope.

## Required separations

Product-regulatory status is not:
- Civic standing.
- Identity verification.
- Participant competence.
- Participant credibility.
- Action authorization.

Ordinary service decisions must not consume irrelevant regulatory fields.

Product-specific restrictions require separate applicable evidence,
reasons, continuity assessment, and review.

## Research

Label-comprehension and communication studies require:
- A completed reference answer key.
- Accessible response methods.
- Separate correctness and confidence measures.
- Explicit treatment of "not sure" and missing responses.
- Prespecified presenter and audience assumptions.
- Preregistered analysis before confirmatory data collection.

## Audit claim

A pass applies only to the declared decision family, fixtures,
observation channels, and execution conditions.

A returned-value audit does not establish absence of logging,
timing, administrative, or cross-service leakage.
