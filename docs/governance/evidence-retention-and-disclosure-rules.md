## Mandatory rules

1. Define the purpose, authority, custodian, sensitivity, retention trigger,
   and review or deletion deadline for each retained evidence class.

2. Collect the minimum evidence needed for the specified institutional
   question. Do not require neural data, diagnoses, private companion
   records, or augmentation inventories by default.

3. Keep personal self-description separate from institutional performance
   measures. Do not create participant ranking, verification, or
   eligibility records from inspection findings.

4. Retention does not authorize reuse, publication, model training,
   onward disclosure, or new sensitive inference.

5. Assess each disclosure for its exact purpose, recipient, content,
   authority, third-party effects, and cumulative release context.

6. Provide affected people accessible reasons and meaningful correction
   routes. Confidentiality restrictions must not become unexplained
   adverse findings.

7. Document preservation holds with authority, scope, permitted uses,
   review dates, and release conditions. Do not use holds as general
   permission for indefinite retention or unrestricted access.

8. Preserve uncertainty. Missing records, stale evidence, and unresolved
   authority must not become participant fault.

9. Apply correction and deletion decisions to known copies and relevant
   derivatives. Explain incomplete propagation and external limits.

10. Keep audit records minimized. Do not reproduce private payloads in
    logs, metric labels, deletion receipts, or public commitments.

11. Record institutional access and release decisions with appropriate
    controls. Evaluate the audit records' own retention and disclosure.

12. Publish institutional performance through reviewed summaries rather
    than unreviewed evidence files.

## Retention outcomes

- retain_within_schedule
- preserve_under_scoped_hold
- deletion_due
- restricted_review_required
- validation_error

deletion_due means schedule processing is required.
It is not proof that deletion has occurred or that every deletion action
is legally authorized.

## Disclosure outcomes

- eligible_for_bound_release
- release_prohibited
- release_review_required
- validation_error

Eligibility is not execution. A release must remain bound to the exact
approved content, recipient, purpose, and applicable limits.

## Accountability

Retention and release decisions require responsible ownership,
accessible correction, conflict-of-interest handling, and escalation.

These rules are proposed project requirements.
Their applicability to real institutional records requires legal,
privacy, security, accessibility, and operational review.
