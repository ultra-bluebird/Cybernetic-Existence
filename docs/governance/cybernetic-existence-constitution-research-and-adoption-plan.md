# Cybernetic Existence Constitution: Research and Adoption Plan

## Status

This document is a governance proposal for Augmented-Hub. It is not legal advice, a statute, a municipal ordinance, or a declaration of authority over non-participating governments, companies, or persons.

## Purpose

Augmented-Hub will use this framework to govern its own services, policies, technical standards, participating-organization agreements, complaint handling, and accountability practices. The framework advances dignity, accessibility, autonomy, privacy, continuity, non-discrimination, civic participation, ecological responsibility, and meaningful human review.

## Scope

This framework applies to:

1. Augmented-Hub-operated services.
2. Personnel, contractors, and systems acting for Augmented-Hub.
3. Participating organizations that execute an adoption agreement.
4. Standards and conformance claims published by Augmented-Hub.

This framework does not replace applicable law, disability accommodations, medical-device regulation, procurement rules, judicial process, or community governance.

## Rights Baseline

The Augmented Bill of Rights is the normative baseline for Augmented-Hub policy. When rights are in tension, decision makers shall apply its published priority order, document the conflict, select the least rights-intrusive feasible option, and provide an accessible explanation and review path.

## Adoption Prerequisites

Adoption requires publication and approval of:

1. A governance charter defining ARC membership, appointment, terms, conflicts of interest, quorum, voting, public-meeting rules, and removal.
2. An ombudsman charter defining independence, complaint intake, confidentiality, investigatory limits, remedies, and external referrals.
3. A consent and sensitive-data policy covering neural, biometric, health, telemetry, augmentation, and identity data.
4. An accessibility and offline-equivalence standard.
5. An interoperability, repair, maintenance, and safe-degraded-mode standard.
6. A decision explanation and appeal standard.
7. A security incident, unsafe-interference, and emergency-response procedure.
8. An ecological-impact measurement and environmental-justice review procedure.
9. A participating-organization adoption agreement.
10. A jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction legal review memorandum before any assertion of legal enforceability.

## Interpretation Rule

Ambiguities in Augmented-Hub policies shall be resolved toward dignity, equal access, data minimization, autonomy, and effective accommodation, provided that the interpretation remains consistent with applicable law, demonstrable safety needs, and the rights of other people.

## Consent Rule

Optional collection, use, sharing, or retention of sensitive data requires explicit, informed, specific, accessible, revocable consent. Essential services shall not be conditioned on consent to unrelated processing, continuous telemetry, proprietary augmentation, or unnecessary external identity verification.

## Review and Remedy

Any materially adverse platform decision must provide:

1. Accessible notice and plain-language reasons.
2. The policy and standards version used.
3. A way to correct material factual errors.
4. Timely human review on request.
5. An independent appeal path where feasible.
6. A documented remedy, limitation, or referral.
7. A privacy-preserving audit record.

## Amendment Controls

Amendments require public notice, an accessible comment period, review by the Advisory Council of Augmented Citizens, a privacy-and-accessibility impact assessment, an ecological-impact assessment where material, and a recorded ARC vote. Emergency changes expire after 30 days unless ratified through the ordinary process.

## Required Impact Record

Each substantive policy, system, or standards decision shall publish:

- knowledge_factor: 0.00 to 1.00
- eco_impact_value: 0.00 to 1.00
- harm_risk: 0.00 to 1.00
- accessibility_impact: 0.00 to 1.00
- rights_alignment: 0.00 to 1.00
- basis: plain-language evidence, uncertainty, affected stakeholders, mitigation, and review status
