Attribution is document credit and workstream metadata only.
It establishes no identity, consent, or execution authority.

## Attribution principles

Assistance does not automatically erase a person's contribution.

Adoption, publication permission, content provenance, verification,
and legal authorship are separate findings.

Ordinary participation must not require a private assistance inventory.

A task-specific disclosure requirement must identify:
- Applicable policy or law.
- Information actually required.
- Purpose and recipient.
- Accessible means of compliance.
- Privacy-preserving alternatives.
- Correction and appeal route.

Reason:
- adopted_exact_content_for_named_audience
- adoption_not_established
- content_changed
- publication_permission_not_granted
- publication_withdrawn
- task_rule_unresolved

Readiness is not execution authorization or a copyright conclusion.

Explicit withdrawal prevents publication under the withdrawn permission.
Materially changed content requires renewed review.
Silence and communication delay are not adoption.

The final route is not a universal finding of valid consent.

## Privacy

No raw neural data, augmentation inventory, or diagnosis is required by
this contract.

Credit labels are participant-selected.
Assistance metadata must not become a public profile.

Detailed refusal and attribution records require access and retention
rules. Public reporting must be minimized and disclosure-reviewed.

## Research

Initial testing uses fictional cases only.

Participant Delphi, vignette, or economic studies require a completed
protocol, appropriate ethical review, accessible consent, and registration
where required by the approved research plan.

No experiment may impose actual loss of essential assistance.

## Acceptance

Keep separate:
- Policy declared.
- Interface specified.
- Test implemented.
- Test executed.
- Independent review completed.
- Real-world outcome evaluated.

No stage establishes the next automatically.
