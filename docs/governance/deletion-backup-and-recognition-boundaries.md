## Deletion

Every deletion statement must identify its assessed scope.

The institution must distinguish:
- Verified deletion assertions.
- Restricted access.
- Scheduled expiry.
- Reviewed retention.
- Unconfirmed external deletion.
- Unknown disposition.

No deletion fraction proves that the inventory is complete.

Backup restoration must reapply applicable deletion, withdrawal,
correction, and restriction decisions.

Retained deletion-control records must be minimized and separately
governed. They must not recreate the private content.

## Backup and restoration language

Every feature called "memory backup" must state what it stores.

Stored records, reminders, generated reconstructions, software state,
and biological recollections are distinct categories.

Integrity is not truth.
Restoration is not current authorization.
Generated reconstruction is not recovered biological memory.

No feature may imply consciousness backup or identity restoration
without an independently established basis for that specific claim.

## Recognition

Nonbiological civic recognition is a separate constitutional-design
proposal requiring authorized institutions and explicit legal scope.

An interface, product claim, model capability, or host association
does not automatically establish legal personality or citizenship.

The process must not reduce existing participants' dignity, privacy,
communication access, or civic standing.

Public reasoning must not require indiscriminate publication of
private operational or personal evidence.

## Evidence

Structural validation, technical verification, legal authority,
public deliberation, and operational outcomes are separate evidence.

No schema pass or test result establishes the others.
