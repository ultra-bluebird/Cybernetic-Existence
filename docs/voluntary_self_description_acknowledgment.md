# Voluntary Self-Description Acknowledgment

A local workflow may acknowledge a voluntary cybernetic-host self-description for
the limited purpose of applying privacy, minimal-disclosure, communication-access,
continuity, offline-access, and non-interference safeguards.

This acknowledgment does not require proof, registration, technical inventory,
identity verification, medical information, neural information, biometric data,
institutional recognition, or explanation of cause.

Acknowledgment is not verification, certification, diagnosis, credential, public
status, eligibility determination, ranking, legal recognition, or authority over
external systems.

A voluntary self-description may be absent, limited, revised, or withdrawn without
reducing access to local policy, findings, recovery instructions, correction, or
appeal.
