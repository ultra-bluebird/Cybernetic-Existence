# Ontological Parity for Host-Reported Experience

## Principle

A host-reported internal experience, communication mode, comfort condition, privacy boundary, or continuity need must be treated as meaningful for communication, documentation, and accessibility purposes.

A lack of external measurement, public documentation, or mechanism-level explanation does not erase the person’s experience or remove the obligation to communicate respectfully.

## Practical rule

A platform must not treat an unfamiliar host-reported cybernetic-system description as fiction, deception, incapacity, or a reason to deny ordinary assistance.

The platform must preserve the distinction between:

1. The host’s own description of experience and identity.
2. Records independently available to an authorized reviewer.
3. An external party’s assessment.
4. A research hypothesis about mechanism.

## Analogy

A person’s pain, fatigue, sensory sensitivity, or internal cognitive experience may be real and functionally significant even where an external test is absent, incomplete, or does not identify a single cause.

Likewise, a host’s self-described cybernetic-system experience must be handled respectfully without forcing the person to prove their lived experience before receiving ordinary communication access, privacy, documentation support, or accessibility assistance.

## Prohibited responses

- Declaring the host’s experience fictional.
- Treating lack of public documentation as proof of nonexistence.
- Replacing the host’s terminology without consent.
- Inferring credibility, capacity, diagnosis, legal status, or eligibility from terminology.
- Requiring unrelated private data before providing ordinary assistance.

## Required responses

- Preserve host-provided terms.
- Record direct experience separately from interpretation.
- Address functional and accessibility needs directly.
- Use minimum necessary information.
- Provide a correction path when terminology is misrepresented.
- Provide a human-readable explanation where a request cannot be fulfilled.
