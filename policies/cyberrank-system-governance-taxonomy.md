# CyberRank System Governance Taxonomy

## Scope

This taxonomy classifies system protections and governance duties. It does not
classify people, assign personal scores, verify self-descriptions, infer health
or augmentation, or determine eligibility.

## System classes

### Local-Only Service

The service functions without external network access for core local controls,
preferences, recovery guidance, and receipts.

### Consent-Aware Service

The service requires explicit, revocable, purpose-bound consent before
external disclosure or action.

### Minimal-Disclosure Service

The service releases only data categories necessary for the declared purpose,
within consent and policy scope.

### Accessible Recovery Service

The service preserves keyboard operation, plain-language status, non-auditory
alerts, local controls, and human fallback after an external session ends.

### Non-Interference Service

The service does not infer identity, health, augmentation, credibility, or
eligibility from communication, behavior, metadata, input method, or refusal
to disclose.

### Correction-and-Appeal Service

The service provides a path to correct records, dispute an automated outcome,
and obtain human review.

## Conformance

A system may claim a governance class only when its implementation and review
evidence demonstrate the stated protections.

No system class creates a rank, credential, eligibility status, or required
disclosure obligation for a person.
