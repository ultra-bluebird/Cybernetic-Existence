# Private Designation Authority Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines respectful, privacy-preserving handling of a designation that is declared by a person and managed through a private institutional record.

## Core rule

A private designation may be preserved as user-defined or institutionally managed metadata without being treated as a public credential, medical diagnosis, technical certification, eligibility category, or automated decision input.

## Required handling

A platform or service must:

- Preserve the holder's stated designation and preferred terminology.
- Mark the designation as private when the holder requests private handling.
- Avoid ridicule, fictionalization, forced relabeling, or unsupported inferences.
- Request only the minimum information necessary for a specific, stated purpose.
- Provide an accessible correction and appeal path for profile or terminology errors.
- Keep an auditable record of material changes to designation-related processing.

## Prohibited handling

A platform or service must not:

- Publish a private designation to a public registry.
- Infer health, capability, credibility, legal status, institutional affiliation, or eligibility.
- Require disclosure of private criteria as a condition of ordinary respectful communication.
- Use the designation for advertising, surveillance, behavioral profiling, or automated adverse decisions.
- Represent a private designation as externally verified without authorized evidence.

## Limited verification

When a specific decision legitimately requires verification, the request must identify:

1. The decision purpose.
2. The minimum claim needed.
3. The authorized verifier.
4. The retention period.
5. The correction and appeal process.

The verifier must not request unrelated private records.
